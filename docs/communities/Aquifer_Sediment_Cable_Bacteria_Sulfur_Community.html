An oxic-anoxic interface and capillary-fringe sediment community from a tar-oil-contaminated aquifer in Flingern (Düsseldorf, Germany) mediated by long filamentous Desulfobulbaceae cable bacteria that bridge spatially separated anodic sulfide oxidation and cathodic oxygen reduction over 1-2 cm via long-distance electron transfer (LDET), co-occurring with Thiobacillus denitrificans and Ignavibacteria at the upper plume fringe and regenerating sulfate for toluene-degrading sulfate-reducing Desulfobulbaceae (PMID:27058505).

Long-Distance Electron Transfer Sulfide Oxidation and Sulfate Recycling

SYNTROPHY

Filamentous Desulfobulbaceae cable bacteria span the 9-25 mm suboxic zone to couple anodic sulfide oxidation in the sulfidic zone with cathodic oxygen reduction in the oxic zone via long-distance electron transfer (LDET, 1.5 mA m-2), directly regenerating sulfate at the oxic-anoxic interface.

Source Taxon: Desulfobulbaceae

Participating Taxa: Desulfobulbaceae, Thiobacillus denitrificans, Ignavibacteria

Metabolites: sulfide (CHEBI:26822 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), dioxygen (CHEBI:15379 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), sulfate (CHEBI:16189 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • sulfur oxidation (GO:0019417 · PathwayMech)
  • electron transport chain (GO:0022900 · PathwayMech)
Downstream Effects:
→ Sulfate-Reducing Hydrocarbon Plume Fringe Biodegradation

Sulfate regenerated by cable-bacterial anodic sulfide oxidation replenishes the electron acceptor pool for sulfate-reducing toluene biodegradation at the plume fringe.

→ Redox-Gradient Niche Competition with Unicellular Sulfide Oxidizers

Spatial decoupling of anodic sulfide oxidation and cathodic oxygen reduction across 1-2 cm gives filamentous cable bacteria a competitive advantage over unicellular Thiobacillus across fluctuating redox gradients.

Evidence

  • PMID:27058505 - SUPPORT (IN_VITRO)
    "Here we provide primary evidence that such sulfur-oxidizing cable bacteria can also be found at oxic–anoxic interfaces in aquifer sediments, where they provide a means for the direct recycling of sulfate by electron transfer over 1–2-cm distance."

Sulfate-Reducing Hydrocarbon Plume Fringe Biodegradation

CROSS_FEEDING

Sulfate-reducing Desulfobulbaceae at the plume fringe utilize regenerated sulfate to degrade toluene and produce sulfide, which is re-oxidized by cable bacteria or precipitated as FeS.

Source Taxon: Desulfobulbaceae

Metabolites: sulfate (CHEBI:16189 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), sulfide (CHEBI:26822 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • dissimilatory sulfate reduction (GO:0019420 · PathwayMech)

Redox-Gradient Niche Competition with Unicellular Sulfide Oxidizers

COMPETITION

Filamentous Desulfobulbaceae cable bacteria compete with unicellular chemolithoautotrophic sulfide oxidizers such as Thiobacillus at the oxic-anoxic interface by bridging separated electron donor and acceptor zones across fluctuating water tables.

Source Taxon: Desulfobulbaceae

Target Taxon: Thiobacillus denitrificans

Metabolites: sulfide (CHEBI:26822 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • sulfur oxidation (GO:0019417 · PathwayMech)

Evidence
