Polyhydroxyalkanoate-Producing Mixed Microbial Culture Under Phage Predation

Community ID CommunityMech:000468
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment sequencing batch reactor enrichment
ENVO:00002123
Modeled Environment activated sludge
ENVO:00002046

Five parallel 2 L sequencing batch reactors enriched from municipal activated sludge under an aerobic dynamic feeding regime, each on a single volatile fatty acid or lactate as sole carbon source, and followed for 145 days by paired metagenomics and metatranscriptomics. The enriched polyhydroxyalkanoate- producing mixed microbial cultures (PHA-MMCs) are dominated by Azoarcus, Paracoccus, Azomonas, Thauera, and Brevundimonas, with 963 metagenome-assembled genomes and 4,294 phage operational taxonomic units recovered. The curated subject is what the resident phage community does to that bacterial community. Bacterial and phage community turnover track each other closely across time, and structural equation modelling resolves the direction: phage abundance correlates negatively with bacterial alpha diversity and activity, consistent with virulent phages lysing whichever species is currently dominant and vacating the niche for a successor. Dominance therefore alternates rather than settling, even under constant selection — a kill-the-winner regime in an engineered system where the enrichment strategy alone would predict a stable dominant. This is a many-phages-on-many-bacteria (N-N) system in the sense of doi:10.1146/annurev-virology-100424-123645, and the companion record to CommunityMech:000467, which carries the 1-N case under experimental control. Phage-encoded auxiliary metabolic genes for the PHA pathway, including phaC, are transcribed here, so the phages are curated as participants in the community's defining metabolic function and not only as predators on it.

CURATION_TODO OPEN Should the per-reactor, per-timepoint phage-host linkages (for example Brevundimonas with phage 1010E_19 in Reactor A, or the intermittent Paracoccus linkages in Reactor D) be curated as individual LYTIC_INFECTION edges, given that the pOTUs have no stable public identifiers?

The paper reports named phage-host linkages per reactor and per time point in supplementary network figures, and they are the most mechanistically specific content it carries. They are not curated here for two reasons: the phage identifiers (0111B24, 1010E_19) are study-local contig labels with no NCBITaxon or other resolvable grounding, and each linkage holds at some time points and not others, which the schema has no way to qualify. Curating them as unqualified pairwise edges would assert a stability the source explicitly denies.

CURATION_TODO OPEN Is a FunctionalRoleEnum value needed for storage-polymer accumulation, which is the defining function of this community and of PHA-MMCs generally?

Every member here is carried with no `functional_role`. The defining function of these genera is accumulating polyhydroxyalkanoate under alternating feast and famine, and no existing value says that. PRIMARY_DEGRADER was considered and rejected: it means degrading complex substrates, while these organisms grow on single short-chain fatty acids and lactate, so using it would assert a metabolism the source does not report — the same failure the PATHOGEN_ANTAGONIST description records for #298/#301. Leaving the slot empty is correct until a value exists, but it does mean the community's central function is readable only from prose and interactions.

OPEN_QUESTION OPEN Should five reactors differing in carbon source and in dominant genus be one record or five?

The five reactors share an inoculum, a selection strategy, and an operating regime, and the paper analyses them as one system, so they are curated as one community. But the dominant genus differs between them — Azoarcus in A and B, Thauera in E — so the DOMINANT rank carried by all five genera here means "dominant in at least one reactor", not "dominant in the community". A reader taking the five dominants as co-dominant in a single vessel would be misled. Splitting per reactor would fix that and lose the cross-reactor comparison that is the paper's design.
