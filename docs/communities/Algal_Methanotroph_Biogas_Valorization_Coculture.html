Algal-Methanotroph Biogas Valorization Coculture

Community ID CommunityMech:000415
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment laboratory haloalkaliphilic biogas photobioreactor
ENVO:01001405

A laboratory enriched haloalkaliphilic microalga-methanotroph co-culture assembled by mixing a halotolerant methanotrophic consortium with a non-axenic algal culture. Triplicate 2.1 L gas-tight glass bioreactors containing 450 mL 6% NaCl mineral salt medium were supplied with a 70:30 CH4:CO2 biogas headspace under light; after two external O2 additions, the culture maintained methane oxidation via algal O2 production and removed 92% CH4 and 89% CO2 while producing ectoine, hydroxyectoine, and carotenoids. Shotgun metagenomics identified Chlorella vulgaris and Dunaliella viridis as the dominant Chlorophyta, Methylotuvimicrobium as the dominant methanotroph, and Methylophaga plus Methylonatrum as co-dominant methylotrophs.
