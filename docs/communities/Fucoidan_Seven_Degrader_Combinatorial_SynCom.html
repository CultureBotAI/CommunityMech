A defined seven-member marine bacterial SynCom assembled from the fucoidan degraders F12, F40, F56, F94, V25, V69, and V4 to quantify complementary fucose- and rare-sugar-monomer degradation of Fucus vesiculosus fucoidan. Sichert et al. constructed all 127 possible non-empty combinations of these degraders at equal OD600 in MBL medium with 0.2% fucoidan, measured total and monomer-specific degradation by liquid chromatography mass spectrometry, and resolved a functionally synergistic community landscape in which specialized degrader combinations reached up to 97.1% total degradation.

Complementary fucoidan monomer degradation

Across the seven-degrader panel, V4/V25 preferentially degrade fucose, F12/F40/F94 rare sugars, and V69/F56 act as generalists. Genomic predictions were compared with experimental LC-MS monomer depletion. Monomer degradation combines cleavage of fucoidan-bound sugars and uptake, not measured CO2 mineralization. Cell-free residual-fucoidan and purified-enzyme assays support complementary substrate accessibility without requiring contact or small-molecule cross-feeding. Six of nine tested soluble enzymes had detectable activity; this does not establish each enzyme as necessary in every community. Participants identify the panel, not seven obligatory partners in every assay. G88 and the algal substrate producer are not members.

Participating Taxa: Algibacter sp. AS12, Algibacter lectus AS40, Mariniflexile sp. AS56, Zobellia laminariae AS94, Luteolibacter sp. AS25, Roseibacillus persicicus AS69, Lentimonas sp. CC4

Seven-degrader synergistic function landscape

All 127 nonempty combinations of the seven listed degraders were assayed, not a single invariant seven-member community. The reported maximum 97.1% monomer degradation occurred in the five-member F40/F56/V69/F94/V4 subset. Functional synergism, reported for 80% of interactions relative to the resource-partitioning null, is distinct from reciprocal fitness benefit; pairwise outcomes include commensalism, amensalism and competition. The mutualistic V69/G88 pair lies outside this panel. Initial OD600 was 0.01 per strain, so total inoculum varied with richness. These endpoint assays do not isolate a richness-only effect or establish ocean carbon-sequestration rates. The model is fitted prediction, not independent enzyme-necessity evidence.

Participating Taxa: Algibacter sp. AS12, Algibacter lectus AS40, Mariniflexile sp. AS56, Zobellia laminariae AS94, Luteolibacter sp. AS25, Roseibacillus persicicus AS69, Lentimonas sp. CC4
