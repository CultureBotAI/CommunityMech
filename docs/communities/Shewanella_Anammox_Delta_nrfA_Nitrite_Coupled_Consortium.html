Engineered anaerobic batch-culture consortium pairing activated anammox sludge from a full-scale municipal wastewater process with an nrfA-deficient Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 mutant. The Delta nrfA strain was inoculated into nitrate- and ammonium-containing mixed medium with sludge from an up-flow anaerobic sludge blanket reactor; blocking the terminal nitrite-to-ammonium DNRA step favored nitrite accumulation, enriched Candidatus Brocadia anammox bacteria, and made the 5:1 anammox-sludge-to-mutant culture the fastest and highest total-nitrogen removal treatment at 97%.

Knowledge gaps & discussions (1)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which endogenous sludge populations directly exchanged nitrite or electrons with the Delta nrfA S. oneidensis mutant, and does the optimized 5:1 phenotype remain stable in a continuous reactor?

The source profiled the final time point by metagenomics and reported the dominant anammox genus, but it did not isotope-trace nitrate, nitrite, or ammonium flux through the mutant, Brocadia, and endogenous denitrifiers. Its Perspective explicitly calls for long-term continuous trials, gaseous-nitrogen measurements, time-series metagenomics, and isotope labeling to resolve those mechanisms.
