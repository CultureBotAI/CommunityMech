EPS-Conditioned Colonization Surface

COLONIZATION_FACILITATION

Pioneer Diatom Surface Attachment

The two group-A diatoms showed predominantly surface-attached growth at the selected early time point, covering only part of the conditioned surface. This is a diatom-subset attachment outcome; leaving unoccupied area is not evidence that these diatoms facilitated later phototrophs. The canonical diatom descriptor represents this selected subset.

AB Diatom Biofilm Development

After group B joined group A, the combined diatom biofilm showed predominantly attached cells, greater surface coverage and a more heterogeneous architecture at the selected AB observation point. Later incubation increased detached cells and reduced PSII yield. These are combined-group time-course observations, not an isolated demonstration of facilitation by one diatom group or niche partitioning.

Mature Periphyton Establishment

The mature ABC periphyton showed broad surface attachment, high density and relatively high PSII yield at the measured endpoint. Although 26 phototrophic species were introduced, the mature community contained at least 22 detected species and three introduced species were no longer detected. This is a community establishment outcome, not proof that all input members persisted or that assembly order caused ecological facilitation.

Reproducible 3D Biofilm Stressor Response

Independent assemblies reproduced community composition and physical structure. Warming and terbuthylazine altered taxon abundances and reduced biofilm structure and biomass in the reported comparisons. PSII quantum yield was generally similar across treatments, with the reported highest-herbicide exception at the AB stage. Preserved PSII is consistent with, but does not demonstrate, functional redundancy. Neither reproducibility nor these stress phenotypes establishes NICHE_PARTITIONING.
