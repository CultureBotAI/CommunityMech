Wheat DT Rhizosphere SynCom

Ecological State ENGINEERED

ENVO:00005801
Modeled Environment wheat plant-associated environment
ENVO:01001001

A defined sixteen-strain drought-tolerant bacterial synthetic community assembled from wheat-rhizosphere isolates collected at the Global Change Experimental Facility in Bad Lauchstadt. Rigerte et al. selected isolates with overlapping 16S rDNA sequences to drought- or future-climate-associated amplicon sequence variants, cultured the wheat isolate set on YME agar, combined equal proportions of the sixteen single-strain suspensions, and applied the Wheat DT-SynCom to barley seedlings before Blumeria graminis f. sp. hordei challenge and pre-challenge rhizosphere multi-omics assays.
