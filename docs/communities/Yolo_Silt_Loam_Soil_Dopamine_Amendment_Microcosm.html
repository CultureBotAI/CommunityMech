Yolo Silt Loam Soil Dopamine Amendment Microcosm

Community ID CommunityMech:000497
Ecological State PERTURBED
Environment agricultural soil microcosm — ontology label: agricultural soil
ENVO:00002259 · HabitatMech ↗

Native bacterial and archaeal community of an agricultural Yolo silt loam from a UC Davis field, incubated as 20 g microcosms for six weeks with twice-weekly dopamine or norepinephrine amendment (1.5 or 3.0 mg) at about 24.5% water content. Proteobacteria, Firmicutes, Actinobacteria, Acidobacteria, Thaumarchaeota and Bacteroidetes dominated control soil. Of all phyla only Actinobacteria were significantly enriched at the high dose of both catecholamines, more by dopamine; Acidobacteria fell under high norepinephrine. Run alongside the Brachypodium hydroponic SynCom experiment of the same study (CommunityMech:000498).

High-Dose Catecholamine Amendment Enriches Actinobacteria

INCREASES

Among all phyla, only Actinobacteria rose significantly in relative abundance in soil given the high dose (3.0 mg) of dopamine or norepinephrine, with the larger effect from dopamine. Relative 16S abundance after six weeks; it does not show catecholamine use as a substrate or absolute growth.

Participating Taxa: Actinobacteria (Actinomycetota)

Metabolites: dopamine (CHEBI:18243 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), norepinephrine (CHEBI:33569 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Evidence

  • PMID:42660928 - SUPPORT (MESOCOSM)
    "Among all bacterial phyla, only Actinobacteria were significantly enriched in soils treated with both dopamine and norepinephrine at the high dose; however, dopamine had a greater effect than norepinephrine"

High-Dose Norepinephrine Depletes Acidobacteria

DECREASES

Acidobacteria relative abundance fell in soil given high-dose norepinephrine compared with control soil. Relative 16S abundance only.

Participating Taxa: Acidobacteria (Acidobacteriota)

Metabolites: norepinephrine (CHEBI:33569 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Evidence

  • PMID:42660928 - SUPPORT (MESOCOSM)
    "Acidobacteria significantly decreased in the soil treated with the high dose of norepinephrine compared to those in the control soil"
