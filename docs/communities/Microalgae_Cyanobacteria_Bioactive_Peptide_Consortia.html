Microalgae-Cyanobacteria Bioactive Peptide Consortia

Community ID CommunityMech:000390
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment controlled marine flat photobioreactor culture
ENVO:01001405

A bounded controlled-photobioreactor biomass panel comprising two marine microalgae-cyanobacteria consortia supplied by Microalgae Solutions S.L. for protein extraction, LC-MS/MS proteome identification, and Alcalase hydrolysis. Consortium C1 combined Chlorella vulgaris with Arthrospira platensis, while consortium C2 combined Kamptonema sp., Nannochloropsis oculata, Tetraselmis suecica, and Chlorella vulgaris. Both biomasses were grown in flat photobioreactors in modified Guillard F/2 medium, lyophilized, characterized for macromolecular composition, extracted at pH 10/pH 5, and hydrolyzed to evaluate release of antioxidant peptides.
