A H2/CO2-fed methanogenic mixed culture, inoculated from mesophilic anaerobic-digester sludge and cultivated in anoxic serum bottles, that was perturbed with sulfate and magnetite to test how a conductive Fe(II)/Fe(III) oxide reshapes sulfate-stressed anaerobic digestion. Vayena et al. (2026) compared a sulfate-free control with 0.6 and 1.2 g/L sulfate, with and without 10 g/L magnetite. At the moderate sulfate dose, magnetite increased methane production by 19% while retaining sulfate reduction and supporting a genome-resolved cooperative network among Methanoculleus methanogens, putative sulfate-reducing bacteria, Limnochordia homoacetogens, and Dethiobacteria syntrophic acetate oxidizers. Community genome-scale metabolic models for representative MAGs supported acetate, amino-acid, and carboxylate exchange among these guilds, but the paper explicitly leaves direct DIET validation for future experiments.
