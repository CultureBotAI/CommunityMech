SO3-Delta-mtrC-Delta-omcA-BS3 Lithium-ion Battery Bioleaching Consortium

Community ID CommunityMech:000528
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment anaerobic brewing-wastewater NCM bioleaching culture — ontology label: laboratory environment
ENVO:01001405 · HabitatMech ↗

Two-member engineered outer-membrane cytochrome perturbation consortium pairing Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 mutant strain SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA (outer-membrane decaheme c-type cytochrome genes mtrC and omcA deleted via pHG1.0 homologous recombination and transformed with pHG13-Ptac-ribADEHC-irrE, Cmr) with Bacillus subtilis 168 strain BS3 (BS2 carrying P43::irrE on pCE2, Cmr) in Moutai brewing wastewater with 0.2 g L^-1 spent LiNixCoyMn1-x-yO2 (NCM) lithium-ion battery cathode black mass (PMID:42733304). Assembled to dissect the outer-membrane c-type cytochrome conduit of the SO3-BS3 consortium (CommunityMech:000385; 452.90 mW m^-2, 102.4 mg L^-1 leached metals), the SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA-BS3 consortium exhibited a 15.95-fold decrease in microbial fuel cell maximum power density to 28.39 mW m^-2 (Figure 5c) and a 7.64-fold decrease in 14-day total leached Li, Ni, Co, and Mn ions to 13.4 mg L^-1 (Figure 5d), compared with 13.4 mg L^-1 for sole strain BS3 monoculture (CommunityMech:000529).

BS3 Ethanol Detoxification and Riboflavin Shuttle Supply with SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA

In the SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA-BS3 consortium, Bacillus subtilis 168 BS3 oxidizes residual brewing-wastewater ethanol to acetate via the P43::adh-aldH dual-enzyme cascade, secretes riboflavin from glucose as an extracellular electron shuttle, and expresses Deinococcus radiodurans IrrE alongside SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA (which catabolizes lactate and expresses Ptac::ribADEHC-irrE) to maintain syntrophic partner viability during NCM bioleaching.

Participating Taxa: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA, Bacillus subtilis 168 BS3

Metabolites: ethanol (CHEBI:16236 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), acetate (CHEBI:30089 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), riboflavin (CHEBI:17015 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Downstream Effects:
→ Attenuated EET and NCM Metal Oxide Leaching in SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA-BS3 Consortium

Despite intact ethanol removal, riboflavin synthesis, and IrrE-mediated Ni2+/Co2+ tolerance, deletion of outer-membrane decaheme c-type cytochrome genes mtrC and omcA in SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA severely restricts electron delivery from lactate catabolism to NCM and riboflavin, reducing consortium EET power density to 28.39 mW m^-2 and total leached metals to 13.4 mg L^-1.

Attenuated EET and NCM Metal Oxide Leaching in SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA-BS3 Consortium

Deletion of outer-membrane decaheme c-type cytochrome genes mtrC and omcA in SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA disrupted both direct outer-membrane cytochrome electron transfer to NCM and MtrC/OmcA-dependent reduction of the riboflavin shuttle, causing the SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA-BS3 consortium EET maximum power density to drop 15.95-fold to 28.39 mW m^-2 (vs 452.90 mW m^-2 in SO3-BS3, Figure 5c) and 14-day total leached Li, Ni, Co, and Mn ions to drop 7.64-fold to 13.4 mg L^-1 (vs 102.4 mg L^-1 in SO3-BS3 and 13.4 mg L^-1 in sole strain BS3, Figure 5d).

Participating Taxa: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO3ΔmtrCΔomcA, Bacillus subtilis 168 BS3

Metabolites: riboflavin (CHEBI:17015 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), lithium(1+) (CHEBI:49713 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), nickel(2+) (CHEBI:49786 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), cobalt(2+) (CHEBI:48828 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), manganese(2+) (CHEBI:29035 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)
