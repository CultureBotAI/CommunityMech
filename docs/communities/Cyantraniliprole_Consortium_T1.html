A defined six-member bacterial consortium, designated T1 or Consortium No. 1, constructed from non-antagonistic pesticide-degrading freshwater isolates enriched near the Kafr El-Zayat Pesticides and Chemicals Factory (Egypt) and evaluated for cyantraniliprole and diamide-insecticide biodegradation. The consortium comprises Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis Re1, Bacillus cereus Re2, Bacillus pumilus Re3, Bacillus paramycoides Re4, Bacillus mycoides Re5, and Bacillus amyloliquefaciens Re6. In 20-day, 30 °C NPK-supplemented simulated contaminated-water microcosms (500 mL, nominal 60 mg/L cyantraniliprole, 57.99 mg/L zero-day residue at 96.65% recovery), T1 achieved 92.00% cyantraniliprole removal, reducing residual cyantraniliprole to 4.64 mg/L while reaching 5.86 x 10^6 CFU/mL on day 11 and decolorizing DCPIP in 27 h.

T1 distributed cyantraniliprole enzyme potential

Post-assay bioinformatic analysis (Table 8 / Figure 5) assigns the six T1 strains a combined repertoire of 19 predicted enzyme hits (covering all five proposed enzyme classes: triosephosphate isomerase, aminopeptidase, catechol 1,2-dioxygenase, catechol O-methyltransferase [COMT in Re1 and Re3], and 2-oxopent-4-enoate hydratase [OEX in Re2]). These represent predicted pathway and enzyme-class coverage rather than measured enzyme expression, catalytic activity, or member-resolved metabolic flux, and do not establish reciprocal fitness benefits.

HPLC-confirmed cyantraniliprole removal

In NPK-amended 500 mL freshwater microcosms incubated at 30 °C for 20 days, T1 bioaugmentation was associated with HPLC-measured cyantraniliprole decline from a 57.99 mg/L zero-day residue (96.65% recovery of the nominal 60 mg/L spike) to 4.64 mg/L after 20 days, corresponding to 92.00% removal and outperforming uninoculated controls C1 and C2. Treatment-level parent-compound loss in non-sterile freshwater microcosms does not establish complete mineralization, detoxification, member-specific flux, or enzyme mediation.

Participating Taxa: Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis Re1, Bacillus cereus Re2, Bacillus pumilus Re3, Bacillus paramycoides Re4, Bacillus mycoides Re5, Bacillus amyloliquefaciens Re6
