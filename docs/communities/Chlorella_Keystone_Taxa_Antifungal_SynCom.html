A four-isolate bacterial synthetic community selected from Chlorella-associated plant microbiome networks: Pseudomonas 8C3D12, Pseudoduganella rivuli FT92W (KACC 21474), Pseudomonas 6C7F4 and Brevibacterium renqingii REN4 (KCTC 49366). The two Pseudomonas isolates share a genus-level taxonomy entry here; three bacterial taxonomy entries do not mean three isolates. Table 1 distinguishes the network label Duganella from the inoculated Pseudoduganella strain. Treat8 combines all four bacteria with Fusarium challenge; Treat9 also contains C. fusca CHK0059. Host disease-index responses depend on treatment, host and time, without demonstrated universal superiority to single isolates or formal synergy. Separate in-vitro strawberry rhizosphere microbiome-fraction assays show compositional responses to live/killed Chlorella, methanol extract and mannitol. Algal mannitol secretion, strain-specific recruitment and the mechanism mediating plant protection remain unresolved. The 2025 precursor names Rhizomicrobium rather than Brevibacterium as its third correlated genus; the two studies do not establish identical keystone sets.

Chlorella-Substance Microbiome Restructuring

In-vitro strawberry rhizosphere microbiome fractions changed composition and diversity during 12- and 36-hour exposure to live or sterilized CHK0059, methanol extract or D-mannitol. Responses differed by substance and time. Only Pseudomonas among the selected network groups was detected in these fractions; this is not demonstrated commensalism, in-plant recruitment or transfer from CHK0059 to the inoculated strains.

Participating Taxa: Chlorella fusca CHK0059, Pseudomonas

Keystone-Taxa SynCom Suppression of Fusarium oxysporum

PARTIAL - the four bacterial isolates, alone or in mixtures and sometimes with CHK0059, altered symptom-based Fusarium wilt disease indices in strawberry and tomato. Treat8 contains all four bacteria plus pathogen; Treat9 additionally contains CHK0059. Results depend on arm, host and time; the full mixture does not uniformly outperform each single isolate. Disease reduction is not a fungal-load measurement or demonstrated competition, direct antibiosis, host-defense activation or formal synergy.

Participating Taxa: Pseudomonas, Pseudoduganella rivuli FT92W, Brevibacterium, Fusarium oxysporum, Chlorella fusca CHK0059

Keystone Plant-Growth-Promoting Trait Pool

PARTIAL - separate isolate screens found protease activity for 8C3D12; phosphate-solubilization, protease, CAS and Nfb responses for 6C7F4; CAS and Nfb responses for FT92W; and CAS plus Salkowski responses for REN4. Supplementary Table S2 additionally reports a positive FT92W Salkowski/IAA screen. Nfb color change is not measured nitrogen flux, and Salkowski color is not chemically resolved IAA. These screens do not establish colonization facilitation, community complementarity or necessary mediation of plant protection.

Strawberry Fusarium Wilt Disease-Index Reduction

Under F. oxysporum f. sp. fragariae F9 challenge, the four-bacterium Treat8 had about 20% strawberry wilt disease index at week 5 versus about 60% for pathogen-only F, consistent with Figure 4. Treat9 adds CHK0059. Table S3 reports Treat8 mean shoot weight 1.47 g versus 0.43 g for F, while the single-isolate Treat3 mean was 2.24 g. These are host symptom and growth endpoints, not proof of direct fungal inhibition or universal mixture superiority.

Tomato Fusarium Wilt Disease-Index Reduction

Under F. oxysporum f. sp. lycopersici challenge, the Results report week-4 mean tomato wilt disease indices of 25% for four-bacterium Treat8 and 18% for Treat9 with CHK0059, below pathogen-only F (about 68-75%). Single-isolate FT92W Treat2 is reported at 22.2%; Figure 5 shows overlapping post-hoc groups among effective arms. Thus lower disease severity does not establish universal full-mixture superiority, direct antagonism, competition or a specific host-defense mechanism.

D-mannitol Enrichment of Pseudomonas in the Chlorella Phytomicrobiome

In-vitro rhizosphere microbiome-fraction assays showed higher Pseudomonas relative abundance with exogenous 2% D-mannitol at 36 hours; Figure S4 also shows lower relative abundance in live and sterilized CHK0059 arms. This compositional response is not absolute Pseudomonas growth, recruitment of 8C3D12 or 6C7F4 in plants, demonstrated algal mannitol secretion, or commensalism. A carbon-source mechanism is HYPOTHESIZED, not traced algal-to-bacterial transfer.

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which microbial or host mechanisms mediate the treatment-specific disease reductions?

Separate microbiome-fraction responses, isolate proxy screens and plant symptoms do not establish a causal chain. No reviewed selective mediator loss/rescue, strain-resolved transfer, fungal-load assay or host-defense perturbation isolates the mechanism. The source body has inconsistent week-7 strawberry wording and supplement figure numbering; use the actual treatment captions/plots and distinguish week-specific values from overall summaries. Methods sample three plants for growth phenotypes while Tables S3/S4 label n=10; inferential precision should not be reconstructed from these summaries.
