SO2-BS1 Lithium-ion Battery Bioleaching Consortium

ENVO:01001405 · HabitatMech ↗

An intermediate two-member engineered Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO2 and Bacillus subtilis 168 BS1 synthetic division-of-labor consortium constructed by Zhang et al. 2026 to evaluate the effect of S. oneidensis riboflavin electron-shuttle overexpression on spent LiNixCoyMn1-x-yO2 (NCM) lithium-ion battery cathode black-mass bioleaching in Moutai brewing wastewater. By pairing S. oneidensis SO2 (carrying the Bacillus subtilis 168 Ptac::ribADEHC operon on pHG13) with ethanol-detoxifying B. subtilis BS1 (carrying P43::adh-aldH on pCE2), the SO2-BS1 consortium synthesized 3.65 uM riboflavin in brewing wastewater (13.22-fold higher than SO1-BS1 at 0.276 uM), achieved a maximum microbial fuel cell power density of 243.71 mW m^-2 (2.10-fold higher than SO1-BS1 at 116.15 mW m^-2), and leached 64.1 mg L^-1 total dissolved Li, Ni, Co, and Mn (53.1, 4.7, 1.9, and 4.4 mg L^-1; leaching efficiencies 66.5%, 18.6%, 20.2%, and 33.3%; maximal leaching rate 16.4 mg L^-1 day^-1) over 14 days before accumulated Ni2+ and Co2+ cytotoxicity lowered the average leaching rate from 9.867 to 0.617 mg L^-1 day^-1 between days 1-6 and days 7-14 (PMID:42733304).

SO2 Ptac::ribADEHC Riboflavin Overproduction and BS1 Ethanol Detoxification

Co-culturing Ptac::ribADEHC-overexpressing Shewanella oneidensis SO2 with P43::adh-aldH-expressing Bacillus subtilis BS1 in Moutai brewing wastewater combines BS1 Adh-AldH ethanol detoxification (converting inhibitory 1.8% v/v residual ethanol via acetaldehyde to acetate) with 13.22-fold higher secreted riboflavin (3.65 uM in SO2-BS1 vs 0.276 uM in SO1-BS1, Figure 3b), elevating microbial fuel cell maximum power density 2.10-fold from 116.15 mW m^-2 (SO1-BS1) to 243.71 mW m^-2 (Figure 3c).

Participating Taxa: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO2, Bacillus subtilis 168 BS1

Metabolites: riboflavin (CHEBI:17015 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), ethanol (CHEBI:16236 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), acetate (CHEBI:30089 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Downstream Effects:
→ SO2-BS1 Riboflavin-Accelerated NCM Metal Oxide Leaching and Late-Stage Rate Decline

Elevating secreted riboflavin 13.22-fold (to 3.65 uM) and EET power density 2.10-fold (to 243.71 mW m^-2) in SO2-BS1 increased 14-day NCM metal oxide leaching 1.26-fold to 64.1 mg L^-1 (53.1 mg L^-1 Li, 4.7 mg L^-1 Ni, 1.9 mg L^-1 Co, 4.4 mg L^-1 Mn) and maximal leaching rate 1.33-fold to 16.4 mg L^-1 day^-1 relative to SO1-BS1 (50.7 mg L^-1, 12.3 mg L^-1 day^-1), before accumulated dissolved Ni2+ and Co2+ depressed the day 7-14 average leaching rate from 9.867 to 0.617 mg L^-1 day^-1.

SO2-BS1 Riboflavin-Accelerated NCM Metal Oxide Leaching and Late-Stage Rate Decline

Catalyzed by the SO2-BS1 consortium in Moutai brewing wastewater with 0.2 g L^-1 spent NCM cathode black mass over 14 days, total dissolved metal ions reached 64.1 mg L^-1 (1.26-fold higher than SO1-BS1 at 50.7 mg L^-1, Figure 3d) with a maximal metal oxide leaching rate of 16.4 mg L^-1 day^-1 (1.33-fold higher than SO1-BS1 at 12.3 mg L^-1 day^-1, Figure S2). Element-resolved leached concentrations reached 53.1 mg L^-1 Li (66.5%), 4.7 mg L^-1 Ni (18.6%), 1.9 mg L^-1 Co (20.2%), and 4.4 mg L^-1 Mn (33.3%) (Figure 3e-h). As leached Ni2+ and Co2+ accumulated, the average leaching rate of SO2-BS1 dropped from 9.867 mg L^-1 day^-1 during days 1-6 to 0.617 mg L^-1 day^-1 during days 7-14 (Figure 4a).

Knowledge gaps & discussions (1)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Why did increasing riboflavin 13.22-fold from 0.276 uM (SO1-BS1) to 3.65 uM (SO2-BS1) capture most of the pre-IrrE leaching gain (50.7 to 64.1 mg L^-1; maximal rate 12.3 to 16.4 mg L^-1 day^-1) whereas a further 12.60-fold riboflavin increase in SO2-BS2 (46.00 uM) yielded only 69.6 mg L^-1 (maximal rate 16.2 mg L^-1 day^-1)?

PMID:42733304 showed that SO2-BS1 (3.65 uM riboflavin, 243.71 mW m^-2) already reached a maximal leaching rate of 16.4 mg L^-1 day^-1 (vs 16.2 mg L^-1 day^-1 in SO2-BS2 at 46.00 uM riboflavin) and experienced a sharp drop in average leaching rate from 9.867 mg L^-1 day^-1 (days 1-6) to 0.617 mg L^-1 day^-1 (days 7-14), indicating that once riboflavin reaches the micromolar range in SO2-BS1, accumulated Ni2+ and Co2+ cytotoxicity rather than electron-shuttle concentration becomes the primary rate-limiting bottleneck.
