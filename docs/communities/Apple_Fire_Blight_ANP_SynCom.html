Apple Fire Blight A+N+P SynCom

ENVO:01001001

A nine-member bacterial synthetic community assembled from three apple fire-blight biocontrol design groups: Streptomyces recifensis SN1E1 and Paenibacillus polymyxa AF2927 as direct anti-Erwinia amylovora strains, Labrys miyagiensis NBRC101365, Labrys okinawensis DSM18385, Novosphingobium mathurense SM117, Novosphingobium endophyticum EGI60015, and Terriglobus aquaticus 03SUJ4 as healthy apple network taxa, and Kitasatospora papulosa AF6313 plus Pseudomonas lundensis AB23 as pathway-associated strains. The full A+N+P mixture eliminated visible disease in Fuji apple fruit wounds, matched oxytetracycline at 1.67% disease severity in rose flowers, and reduced Erwinia amylovora TS3128 density in apple flower exudate during follow-up co-cultivation assays.

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Does Terriglobus aquaticus TAH1 in the follow-up genome paper represent the same strain as Terriglobus aquaticus 03SUJ4 in the SynCom construction paper?

The construction paper's membership table names Terriglobus aquaticus strain 03SUJ4, but the genome table in the follow-up paper names the analyzed Terriglobus aquaticus strain TAH1.

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which predicted secondary-metabolite clusters are experimentally responsible for Erwinia amylovora suppression by the non-A network and pathway SynCom members?

The follow-up paper predicted secondary-metabolite clusters from the network and pathway strain genomes, but Supplementary Table 1 was available only as a PDF/image supplement during curation and the publication does not chemically validate member-level effectors for the full A+N+P community.
