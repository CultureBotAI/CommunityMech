Soy Sauce Temporal Seven-Species SynCom

Community ID CommunityMech:000378
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment controlled soy sauce moromi fermentation
ENVO:03600040

A laboratory-scale temporal synthetic microbial community for soy sauce moromi fermentation. Chen et al. 2026 distilled a nine-member candidate set to seven core species and tested delayed inoculation schemes in which Aspergillus oryzae 3.042 made koji, Weissella paramesenteroides WP-8 was added at day 0, Lactiplantibacillus plantarum JL-20 at day 5, Pediococcus pentosaceus PP-11 plus Tetragenococcus halophilus R44 at day 10, and Zygosaccharomyces rouxii ZQ-01 plus Candida orthopsilosis CV-13 at day 25. The best W->L->P+T->Z+C formulation raised glutamic acid, 4-ethylguaiacol, and total volatile production relative to the traditional-fermentation control under homogeneous 40-day laboratory conditions.
