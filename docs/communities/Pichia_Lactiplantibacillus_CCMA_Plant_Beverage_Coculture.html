Pichia-Lactiplantibacillus CCMA Plant Beverage Coculture

Community ID CommunityMech:000345
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment defined plant-based beverage fermentation — ontology label: laboratory environment
ENVO:01001405

A defined bacterial-yeast co-culture pairing Lactiplantibacillus plantarum CCMA 0743 with Pichia kluyveri CCMA 0615 to ferment a non-dairy beverage made from oats, almonds, and sunflower seeds. After 24 h of fermentation both partners remained highly viable, with 9.32 log CFU/mL L. plantarum and 7.05 log CFU/mL P. kluyveri. The co-culture acidified the plant-based substrate, utilized carbohydrates, generated bioactive metabolites, increased linoleic acid, short-chain fatty acids, phenolic acids, vitamins B2 and B12, and GABA, and its fermented beverage promoted gut-microbiota recovery in a BALB/c mouse vancomycin-induced dysbiosis model.
