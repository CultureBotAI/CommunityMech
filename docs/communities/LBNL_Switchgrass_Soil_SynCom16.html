KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which interactions explain diversity responses within each defined SynCom variant?

Automated assembly and preservation are methodological results, not ecological causal arrows. Earlier nutrient, ratio and plant observations survive in the three discussions below; their 18/17-member panels cannot be mapped to this final 16 collection by deleting a domain placeholder. The final Methods list and Table S1 disagree, so stock correspondence across panels remains uncertain. Taxon dropout results and proposed Lysobacter facilitation/fast-grower competition are separate observations or interpretations, not newly inferred partner-specific arrows. Relative sequencing, PMA and DNA spike-ins do not establish absolute viable counts for every stock. Main text, original figures 2/3/5 and separate Table S1 were inspected in this follow-up; previous immutable reviews remain preserved.

INTERPRETATION OPEN Low-Nutrient Alpha-Diversity Enrichment: source-scoped observation in earlier assay panels.

In the initial equally mixed 18-strain community, dilution from 1X to 0.2X or 0.1X R2A lowered total OD600 while increasing Shannon diversity and evenness after three days. Pseudomonas remained relatively dominant. This assay preceded the final 16-member model and does not demonstrate increased absolute viable abundance, partner-specific facilitation or the same response in every community variant. Main Methods/Table S1 stock discrepancies prevent assigning this observation to the complete Methods-defined final 16 roster. The observation is retained; its graph interpretation for all final 16 members is withheld.

INTERPRETATION OPEN Starting-Ratio Dependent Community Diversity: source-scoped observation in earlier assay panels.

Adjusting inoculum ratios to favor organisms that became relatively rare in equal mixtures increased Shannon diversity and evenness at two and six days in the tested panels, particularly 2X/3X cutoff communities without Pseudomonas. These are defined starting-composition effects, not a universal optimum or proof of a unique competition mechanism. INTERPRETATION OPEN EcoFAB Brachypodium Rhizosphere Colonization: source-scoped observation in earlier assay panels.

Equal mixtures of 17 or 18 isolates, without or with Pseudomonas, were inoculated onto 12-day-old Brachypodium in EcoFABs and profiled seven days later. Root-associated community composition differed from the inoculum. These are plant-colonization observations, not demonstrated reciprocal benefit; the single inoculum batch limits taxon-level before/after significance tests. Pseudomonas presence did not significantly change overall EcoFAB composition, unlike the in-vitro diversity response.
