A thermophilic, anaerobic, semi-continuously fed methanogenic enrichment microbiome cultivated on mid-season switchgrass at high solids loadings. The community maintained fractional carbohydrate solubilization as solids loading increased from 30 to 150 g/L, with methane and carbon dioxide as the main steady-state products. Metaproteomics identified bacterial carbohydrate-active enzyme strategies and archaeal methanogenesis functions relevant to DOE bioenergy efforts to understand robust lignocellulose deconstruction under industrially relevant process conditions.

High-Solids Switchgrass Carbohydrate Solubilization

The thermophilic HR1 community maintained fractional carbohydrate solubilization as switchgrass loading increased from 30 to 150 g/L in one sequentially operated reactor. This positive bulk-carbohydrate result does not demonstrate complete lignin mineralization or selectively identify the causal enzyme strategy. Vitamins, medium optimization, mixing and adaptation also changed during operation. LC-MS profiles identify expressed protein groups with annotated CAZyme potential, not direct catalytic activities; the five canonical taxa are a curated subset, not every organism responsible for conversion.

Fraction-Associated CAZyme Protein Profiles

Chloroflexi-assigned CAZyme proteins were more prevalent in the planktonic-cell fraction, whereas Thermotogae-assigned proteins were more represented in supernatant and substrate-bound fractions. These positive metaproteomic profiles motivated the authors' functional-niche interpretation. Centrifugation-enriched fractions and protein adsorption do not directly locate every living organism, establish competitive niche partitioning, or demonstrate a Chloroflexota-to-Thermotogota interaction.

Participating Taxa: Chloroflexota, Thermotogota

Methane Production and Inferred Hydrogenotrophic Route

Methane and carbon dioxide were measured reactor products. The higher abundance and diversity of proteins assigned to hydrogenotrophic rather than acetoclastic pathways suggest a major hydrogenotrophic route, but do not quantify its fraction of methane production or exclude other routes. Steady-state hydrogen and volatile fatty acids below detection do not mean absent turnover. The canonical archaeal group represents inferred methanogenic capacity, not the complete community or an experimentally resolved partner pair.

Knowledge gaps & discussions (1)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which enzymes and metabolic routes maintain conversion at high solids?

One sequential HR1 reactor supports positive conversion and gas outcomes but does not isolate solids loading from vitamin, medium, mixing or adaptation changes. Metaproteomic replicates are not independent replicate reactors, and the sequence database was derived from lower-solids metagenomes. Selective enzyme perturbations, catalytic assays and route-resolved flux would test both existing arrows. AA6 enrichment motivated an oxidative/Fenton accessibility hypothesis, but ROS, iron cycling and lignin-depolymerization flux were not directly established in the inspected study; no new oxidative branch is asserted. The authors' proposed syntrophic acetate oxidation likewise remains unresolved. Canonical broad taxa are incomplete and are not a newly grounded strain roster.
