A laboratory microbial consortium enriched from activated sludge sampled at the San Jerónimo WWTP in Seville, Spain, by diclofenac selection and a glucose-supplemented MSM subculture. The MC degraded all 10 mg/L diclofenac in a glucose-amended mineral-salts flask assay within 5 days, producing extracellular 4'-hydroxy-diclofenac, 5-hydroxy-diclofenac, and putative nitro-DCF. 16S and ITS metabarcoding at the beginning and after 28 days showed a shift from Burkholderia-, Klebsiella-, Ochrobactrum-, Chthoniobacter-, and Malassezia-rich starting profiles to final bacterial and fungal profiles dominated by Burkholderia and Talaromyces, respectively.
