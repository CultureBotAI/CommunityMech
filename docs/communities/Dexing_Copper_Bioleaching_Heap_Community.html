Dexing Copper Mine Bioleaching Heap Surface Community

Community ID CommunityMech:000485
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment mine tailing
ENVO:00000003 · HabitatMech ↗

An acidophilic chemolithoautotrophic microbial community inhabiting the surface layer of industrially bioleached low-grade copper tailings at the Dexing Copper Mine heap (Jiangxi Province, China), dominated by Acidithiobacillus ferrivorans-like and Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans-like populations alongside Thiobacillus-like and Leptospirillum ferrooxidans-like guilds that partition Calvin-Benson-Bassham and rTCA carbon fixation, nitrogen fixation, ferrous iron oxidation, sulfur oxidation, and hydroxyl radical detoxification (PMID:26781463, doi:10.1186/s12863-016-0330-4).

Cooperative Chemolithoautotrophic Carbon and Nitrogen Fixation

MUTUALISM

Acidithiobacillus, Thiobacillus, and Leptospirillum ferrooxidans partition autotrophic carbon fixation (Rubisco and PRK in the CBB cycle alongside rTCA enzymes) and nitrogen fixation/assimilation to sustain primary production in nutrient-poor acidic copper tailings.

Source Taxon: Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans

Participating Taxa: Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans, Thiobacillus, Leptospirillum ferrooxidans

Metabolites: carbon dioxide (CHEBI:16526 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), nitrate (CHEBI:17632 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • carbon fixation (GO:0015977 · PathwayMech)
  • nitrogen fixation (GO:0009399 · PathwayMech)
Downstream Effects:
→ Coupled Ferrous Iron and Reduced Sulfur Oxidation in Copper Tailings

Autotrophic carbon and fixed nitrogen supply supports chemolithotrophic iron- and sulfur-oxidizing guilds across the bioleaching heap surface.

Coupled Ferrous Iron and Reduced Sulfur Oxidation in Copper Tailings

SYNTROPHY

Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans, Acidithiobacillus (including A. ferrivorans-like populations), and Thiobacillus oxidize ferrous iron and reduced inorganic sulfur compounds (via SQR, TQO, TetH, truncated SoxAX/B/YZ, and APS reductase aprAB) to drive sulfide mineral dissolution in the copper bioleaching heap.

Source Taxon: Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans

Participating Taxa: Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans, Acidithiobacillus, Thiobacillus

Metabolites: iron(2+) (CHEBI:29033 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), iron(3+) (CHEBI:29034 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), thiosulfate(2-) (CHEBI:16094 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), sulfate (CHEBI:16189 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • sulfur oxidation (GO:0019417 · PathwayMech)
  • electron transport chain (GO:0022900 · PathwayMech)
Downstream Effects:
→ Peroxiredoxin-Mediated Hydroxyl Radical Detoxification

Aerobic Fe(II) oxidation in acidic tailings generates Fenton-type reactive oxygen stress that requires community peroxiredoxin detoxification.

Peroxiredoxin-Mediated Hydroxyl Radical Detoxification

MUTUALISM

Leptospirillum ferrooxidans-like populations supply peroxiredoxin genes (LcPxn1, LcPxn3, LdPxn1) to detoxify hydroxyl radicals generated by Fenton reactions of ferrous iron with oxygen during acid bioleaching.

Source Taxon: Leptospirillum ferrooxidans

Metabolites: iron(2+) (CHEBI:29033 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), dioxygen (CHEBI:15379 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • cellular oxidant detoxification (GO:0098869 · PathwayMech)
  • response to oxidative stress (GO:0006979 · PathwayMech)

Evidence

  • doi:10.1186/s12863-016-0330-4 - SUPPORT (FIELD_STUDY)
    "Based on sequence identities (30 % identity cut-off; E-value ≤1e−5), sequence homologs for those peroxiredoxin coding genes (LcPxn1, LcPxn3 and LdPxn1) were mostly assigned to a Leptospirillum-like populations, indicating its key role in the detoxification of hydroxyl radicals."
