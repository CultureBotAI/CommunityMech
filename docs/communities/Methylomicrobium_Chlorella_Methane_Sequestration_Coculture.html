Methylomicrobium-Chlorella Methane Sequestration Coculture

Community ID CommunityMech:000192
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment saline methane-fed laboratory coculture
ENVO:01001405

A defined halotolerant methanotroph-microalga coculture pairing Methylomicrobium alcaliphilum 20Z, currently represented by NCBI as Methylotuvimicrobium alcaliphilum 20Z, with Chlorella sp. HS2. The two-member system was assembled to test biological methane and carbon dioxide mitigation through metabolic coupling in saline culture, with increased methane removal, improved growth of both members, pH recovery, and greater biomass carbon sequestration reported in coculture. The system is relevant to DOE interests in methane mitigation, carbon capture and utilization, photosynthetic-methanotrophic consortia, and C1 bioconversion.
