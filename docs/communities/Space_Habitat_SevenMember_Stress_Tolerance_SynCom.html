Space Habitat Seven-Member Stress-Tolerance SynCom

Community ID CommunityMech:000339
Ecological State ENGINEERED

ENVO:01001405

A defined seven-member bacterial consortium assembled as a tractable space-habitat microbiome model from ISS-derived, spacecraft-associated, and biosafety-level-1 strains of genera commonly detected in spacecraft. The consortium contained Staphylococcus capitis K1-2-2-23, Bacillus subtilis W2-B1-K4, Pseudomonas antarctica CMS 35T, Streptococcus halotolerans HTS9T, Propionibacterium cyclohexanicum TA-12T, Micrococcus luteus W4-Al-K1, and Acinetobacter radioresistens 50V1. Equal OD-adjusted suspensions of the seven species were pooled to OD600 0.125 and used for static co-cultivation, early biofilm biomass, desiccation, X-ray irradiation, hydrogen peroxide, and pilot 16S rRNA gene sequencing assays. The study quantified stress survival of B. subtilis and S. capitis as single species and inside the full consortium; consortium context increased B. subtilis survival after 500 Gy X-ray irradiation but lowered recoverable B. subtilis CFU after 24 h co-cultivation in the full community.
