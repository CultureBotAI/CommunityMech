Bacillales Lignin-Degrading LDSynCom

Community ID CommunityMech:000383
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment M9 beechwood-lignin laboratory medium
ENVO:01001405

An engineered eleven-strain Bacillales synthetic community assembled for aerobic beechwood-lignin degradation in M9 minimal medium. Zhou et al. 2026 screened a 121-strain Bacillales collection with a bottom-up clear-halo lignin-utilization assay and a top-down M9-lignin enrichment, distilled 15 candidates with crystal-violet biofilm and methylene-blue/Azure-B dye-decolorization assays, and retained 11 strains for the lignin-degrading SynCom. The final LDSynCom formed biofilms on lignin particles, accumulated more Folin-Ciocalteu-reactive phenolics than uninoculated and 11B-dropout controls, and produced extracellular proteomes whose lignin-induced proteins included Paenibacillus sp. 11B ring-cleaving dioxygenase candidates.
