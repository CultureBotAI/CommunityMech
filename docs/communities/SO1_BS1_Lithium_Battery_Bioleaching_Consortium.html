SO1-BS1 Lithium-ion Battery Bioleaching Consortium

Community ID CommunityMech:000519
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment anaerobic brewing-wastewater NCM bioleaching culture — ontology label: laboratory environment
ENVO:01001405 · HabitatMech ↗

A two-member engineered Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO1 and Bacillus subtilis 168 BS1 synthetic division-of-labor consortium for metal oxide leaching from spent LiNixCoyMn1-x-yO2 (NCM) lithium-ion battery cathode black mass using Moutai brewing wastewater as the organic leaching medium. In the baseline SO1-BS1 consortium, B. subtilis BS1 (carrying P43::adh from Saccharomyces cerevisiae BY4341 and aldH from Issatchenkia terricola XJ-2 on pCE2) removes 97.6% of residual ethanol in brewing wastewater within 48 h via an Adh-AldH dual-enzyme cascade, relieving ethanol inhibition of S. oneidensis SO1 extracellular electron transfer (increasing microbial fuel cell maximum power density to 116.15 mW m^-2) and leaching 50.7 mg L^-1 total dissolved Li, Ni, Co, and Mn (45.6, 2.4, 1.0, and 1.7 mg L^-1) over 14 days with XPS-verified reduction of Mn4+/Mn3+, Co3+, and Ni3+ to Mn2+, Co2+, and Ni2+ (PMID:42733304).

BS1 Adh-AldH ethanol detoxification relieving SO1 EET inhibition

In the SO1-BS1 consortium, Bacillus subtilis 168 BS1 oxidizes residual ethanol (1.8% v/v) in Moutai brewing wastewater via acetaldehyde to acetate through heterologous P43::adh-aldH, removing 97.6% of ethanol within 48 h and increasing microbial fuel cell maximum power density to 116.15 mW m^-2 (34.47-fold higher than SO1 alone at 3.10 mW m^-2 and 92.92-fold higher than BS1 alone at 1.25 mW m^-2).

Source Taxon: Bacillus subtilis 168 BS1

Target Taxon: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO1

Metabolites: ethanol (CHEBI:16236 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), acetate (CHEBI:30089 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Downstream Effects:
→ SO1-BS1 extracellular electron transfer reduction of NCM metal oxides

Removal of 97.6% of inhibitory ethanol by BS1 restores SO1 extracellular electron transfer (116.15 mW m^-2 vs 3.10 mW m^-2 in SO1 alone), enabling 3.29-fold higher total NCM metal ion leaching by SO1-BS1 (50.7 mg L^-1) compared with SO1 alone (15.4 mg L^-1).

Evidence

  • PMID:42733304 - SUPPORT (IN_VITRO)
    "It was found that 97.6% of ethanol in the brewing wastewater could be removed by the SO1‐BS1 microbial consortium within 48 h (Figure 2b ). Moreover, the maximum power density of the SO1‐BS1 consortium reached 116.15 mW m−2, representing 34.47‐ and 92.92‐fold increases compared to that observed in strain SO1 (3.10 mW m−2) and BS1 (1.25 mW m−2), respectively (Figure 2c ), confirming that the ethanol inhibition of the EET of the SO1‐BS1 consortium was significantly alleviated."

SO1-BS1 extracellular electron transfer reduction of NCM metal oxides

With ethanol inhibition alleviated by BS1, the SO1-BS1 consortium catalyzes anaerobic brewing-wastewater reduction of high-valence NCM cathode metal oxides (reducing Mn4+/Mn3+ from 68.69%/23.64% to 45.40%/6.80% with Mn2+ rising from 7.67% to 47.80%, reducing Co3+ from 98.06% to almost exclusively Co2+, and reducing Ni3+ from 96.93% to 12.96% with Ni2+ rising to 87.04%), leaching 50.7 mg L^-1 total dissolved metal ions (45.6 mg L^-1 Li, 2.4 mg L^-1 Ni, 1.0 mg L^-1 Co, and 1.7 mg L^-1 Mn) over 14 days.

Participating Taxa: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 SO1, Bacillus subtilis 168 BS1

Metabolites: riboflavin (CHEBI:17015 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), lithium(1+) (CHEBI:49713 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), nickel(2+) (CHEBI:49786 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), cobalt(2+) (CHEBI:48828 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), manganese(2+) (CHEBI:29035 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)
