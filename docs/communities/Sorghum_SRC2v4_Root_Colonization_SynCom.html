A 15-member reduced-complexity Sorghum bicolor synthetic root community rationally derived from the 57-member Sorghum Root Consortium 1 (SRC1). SRC2v4 was designed by ranking SRC1 strains by cumulative root abundance in a prior permutational membership experiment, prioritizing known sorghum root-exudate users when abundance rankings were highly similar, and selecting 15 high-colonization isolates with unique 16S rRNA gene sequences in the V3-V4 region. In gnotobiotic 5-l sterile microboxes, SRC2v4 showed exceptional compositional fidelity relative to its inoculum, stably colonized roots and rhizospheres, promoted plant growth as a live community but not as a heat-killed inoculum, and induced coordinated S. bicolor transcriptional and metabolomic responses centered on flavonoid biosynthesis.
