Clostridium acetobutylicum-Clostridium ljungdahlii Syntrophic Fusion Coculture

Community ID CommunityMech:000336
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment static anaerobic laboratory coculture in loosened screw-cap bottles
ENVO:01001405

A defined two-member anaerobic coculture of the solventogen Clostridium acetobutylicum ATCC 824 and the acetogen Clostridium ljungdahlii DSM 13528. CO2 and H2 produced by C. acetobutylicum feed C. ljungdahlii, and the pairing also produces interspecies cell fusion with large-scale exchange of protein, RNA, and DNA between the two organisms. RNA sequencing of the coculture against the two monocultures, and of membrane-separated against unseparated cocultures in a transwell system, separates the effect of soluble signals from the effect of direct cell-to-cell contact. The transcriptional program shows differential regulation of the C. ljungdahlii Wood-Ljungdahl pathway and of the motility machinery, and of amino-acid biosynthesis and degradation; arginine and histidine made by C. acetobutylicum are proposed to be metabolized by C. ljungdahlii for extra ATP and carbon. The authors read the pairing as mutualistic rather than the commensalism previously assumed. This record is scoped to the WILD-TYPE pairing. The same laboratory has since reported much higher isopropanol and butanol titers from the same species pair using genetically modified C. acetobutylicum; those are properties of the engineered pairing and are deliberately not curated here.
