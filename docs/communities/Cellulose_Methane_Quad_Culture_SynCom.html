Knowledge gaps & discussions (1)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Does D. vulgaris fermentation of lactate to acetate and CO2 causally enhance acetoclastic methanogenesis by M. concilii, i.e. is there a second (D. vulgaris-derived) carbon route to methane beyond the direct R. cellulolyticum → M. concilii hand-off?

If confirmed, D. vulgaris would be a secondary acetate/CO2 supplier to the acetoclastic methanogen, adding a parallel carbon route to methane and changing how the causal graph attributes methane output between the two carbon sources. The source paper proposes it mechanistically ("it may be") but does not demonstrate the D. vulgaris → M. concilii carbon edge directly, so it is recorded here as a knowledge gap rather than asserted as an ecological interaction.
