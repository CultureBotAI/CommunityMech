Baijiu Pit Mud Hexanoic Acid SynCom G4

Community ID CommunityMech:000365

Modeled Environment Chinese baijiu fermentation pit mud

An ethanol-fed, hexanoic-acid-producing synthetic community constructed from Chinese baijiu fermentation pit mud using a top-down strategy. The G4 SynCom was dominated by Clostridium sensu stricto 12, primarily Clostridium kluyveri at 86%, with Clostridium sensu stricto 18 and Pseudoclavibacter also reported among dominant prokaryotes. Functional predictions and fermentation profiling linked the G4 community to reverse beta-oxidation-mediated hexanoic acid formation; under optimized pH 7.9, 37.6 C, 8.2% inoculum, and 77% loading-volume conditions, SynCom G4 reached 4.69 g/L hexanoic acid.
