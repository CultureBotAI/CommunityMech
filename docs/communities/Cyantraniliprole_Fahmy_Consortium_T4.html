A defined six-member bacterial consortium, designated T4 or Fahmy-consortia, that was previously characterized for pesticide bioremediation and was re-evaluated as a cyantraniliprole-removal benchmark in simulated contaminated water. The consortium contains Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis AZFS3, Bacillus pumilus AZFS5, Bacillus mojavensis AZFS15, Bacillus paramycoides AZFS18, Pseudomonas aeruginosa KZFS4, and Alcaligenes aquatilis KZFS11. In 20-day, 30 C simulated-water microcosms seeded with standardized bacterial inocula, T4 achieved the highest tested cyantraniliprole removal: HPLC measured 98.27% removal from the 57.79 mg/L zero-day residue and a 1.00 mg/L residue after 20 days in the nominal 60 mg/L treatment.
