The defined 15-stock bacterial inoculum assembled from Brachypodium rhizobiome isolates. In plant experiments it improved measured growth endpoints after drought followed by rewatering; sustained drought did not show those improvements. The administered SynCom is not the complete observed root microbiome, which also contained other bacteria. Liquid-culture persistence was unequal.

Genomic Osmoprotectant and Ion-Transport Prediction

Genome annotations of individual members predict osmoprotectant and ion-transport potential, including trehalose/betaine-related genes. PARTIAL - gene presence does not establish expression, synthesis, secretion, transfer to Brachypodium or necessary mediation of drought recovery. This is member functional potential, not demonstrated interstrain cross-feeding. The panel comprises all 15 sequenced isolates; individual genes and positive traits differ by strain (Table 1), so no trait is assigned uniformly to every member.

Host-Associated Persistence Compared With Liquid Culture

The 15-member inoculum showed more balanced 16S relative profiles with Brachypodium than in liquid culture. In the three-week weekly-transfer 0.2X MS experiment, six strains were undetectable or below 0.1% relative abundance; three taxa dominated. Plant experiments reported persistence of members, with unequal abundances. These sequencing observations do not establish equal stability of all 15 strains in vitro, absolute viable counts or reciprocal mutualism.

Brachypodium Drought Recovery Promotion

HOST_IS_TARGET

SynCom-amended Brachypodium showed better shoot weight, shoot height, root length and leaf-number outcomes after drought followed by rewatering than unamended plants. The Results report no significant differences in these endpoints during sustained drought. PARTIAL - osmoprotectant and ion-transporter genes are proposed contributors, not selectively demonstrated causes of recovery.

Strain-Dependent Root Spatial Distribution

After 21 days, 16S profiles differed between root tips and bases in a strain- and condition-dependent manner. MA-N2 sp. RCC-150 showed root-tip enrichment under drought and salt stress; some other strains were instead enriched at the base. HYPOTHESIZED - exudate-guided recruitment and metabolite return could contribute, but isolate carbon-use assays and spatial relative abundance do not trace migration, exchange or selective mediation of recovery. The 15 entries identify the sequenced panel, not a uniform root-tip preference. No movement of individually tracked cells was measured.
