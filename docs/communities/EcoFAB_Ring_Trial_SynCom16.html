EcoFAB Ring Trial SynCom16

Community ID CommunityMech:000513
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment EcoFAB 2.0 hydroponic device — ontology label: laboratory environment
ENVO:01001405 · HabitatMech ↗

A 16-member synthetic bacterial community (SynCom16) assembled from switchgrass (Panicum virgatum) rhizosphere isolates across 16 genera (omitting the fast-growing Paraburkholderia sp. OAS925 present in SynCom17, CommunityMech:000025) and evaluated across five independent laboratories (n = 7 biological replicates per lab) on Brachypodium distachyon Bd21-3 in EcoFAB 2.0 sterile hydroponic devices. In the absence of Paraburkholderia sp. OAS925, SynCom16 root colonization at 22 days after inoculation was dominated by Rhodococcus sp. OAS809 (68 +- 33% of root reads), Methylobacterium sp. OAE515 (15 +- 20%), and Mycobacterium sp. OAE908 (14 +- 27%), produced reproducible spent-medium exometabolite shifts across participating laboratories, and reduced Brachypodium shoot fresh weight relative to axenic plants without altering root fresh weight.

Rhodococcus-, Methylobacterium-, and Mycobacterium-Dominated SynCom16 Root Assembly

COMPETITIVE_RELEASE

In the absence of the fast-growing competitor Paraburkholderia sp. OAS925, SynCom16 assembled a distinct root-associated community on Brachypodium distachyon at 22 days after inoculation across the five ring-trial laboratories, with Rhodococcus sp. OAE908 to dominate SynCom16 roots when Paraburkholderia sp. OAS925 is absent, and why did Lab E show higher Methylobacterium/Mycobacterium root reads than Labs A-D?

OAE908 (14 +- 27%) on roots and removed the root fresh-weight penalty seen in SynCom17, 16S relative abundance and bulk spent-medium LC-MS/MS profiles do not resolve pairwise cross-feeding among the 16 strains or the specific microbial factor causing shoot fresh-weight reduction.

Growth Media

R2A (bacterial preculture); half-strength Murashige-Skoog (MS) basal salts for the EcoFAB 2.0 plant system

Temperature 27 °C

Preparation notes: SynCom strains were grown from single colonies in R2A liquid at 27 °C (the Paraburkholderia RFP strain grown overnight in R2A shaken at 200 rpm at 27 °C), washed in ½ MS basal salts, and used to inoculate 3-day-old B. distachyon seedlings in EcoFAB 2.0 devices; plants were grown in a chamber at 26 °C (light) / 20 °C (dark) with 110-140 µmol/m²/s PPFD and 70% humidity.
