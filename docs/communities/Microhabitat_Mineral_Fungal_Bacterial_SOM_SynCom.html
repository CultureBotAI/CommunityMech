A four-member artificial soil-organic-matter microcosm inoculum consisting of Curtobacterium sp. PTCC 1316, Trichoderma harzianum IBRC-M30059, Phanerochaete chrysosporium IBRC-M30144, and Pseudomonas fluorescens IBRC-M10752. Maqami et al. 2026 used the synthetic fungi-plus-bacteria consortium as an experimentally simplified alternative to a native agricultural-soil microbiome in 500 mL glass-jar microcosms containing sterilized bentonite or palygorskite clay, calcium carbonate, quartz sand, and wheat-straw or alfalfa-shoot litter, then quantified respiration kinetics and microbial biomass carbon over a 90-day incubation.

Knowledge gaps & discussions (2)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN What fraction of CaCO3-amended CO2 efflux was microbial respiration rather than abiotic carbonate-derived CO2?

The study measured total CO2 efflux but explicitly reports that abiotic CO2 release from CaCO3 was not separated, so carbonate-associated respiration endpoints are curated as net microcosm outcomes.

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which enzyme-sorption or molecular-scale organo-mineral stabilization pathways drove the palygorskite and CaCO3 effects?

The authors interpret respiration and biomass patterns through clay microhabitat engineering, enzyme retention, and stabilization hypotheses, but state that the underlying enzyme-sorption and molecular-scale stabilization pathways were not directly quantified.
