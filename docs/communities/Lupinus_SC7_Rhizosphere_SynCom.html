Lupinus SC-7 Rhizosphere SynCom

A defined twelve-strain bacterial synthetic community, SC-7, assembled from representative Bradyrhizobium, Micromonospora, Caulobacter, Kribbella, Pseudomonas, Pseudoxanthomonas, Rhizobium, Sphingopyxis, Stenotrophomonas, Streptomyces, and Variovorax isolates from the wild Lupinus angustifolius microbiome. The study selected these strains from core-hub-associated, peripheral, and satellite genera in a lupin root co-occurrence network, mixed them at equal optical density for gnotobiotic FlowPot and non-sterile soil inoculation, and found that increasing SynCom complexity reshaped root-associated microbiota while SC-7 gave the strongest lupin growth and transcriptional response among the tested SynComs.
