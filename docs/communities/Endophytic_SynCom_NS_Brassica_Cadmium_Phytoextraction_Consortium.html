Hyperaccumulator-Derived Endophytic SynCom-NS for Brassica juncea Cadmium Phytoextraction

Community ID CommunityMech:000476
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment contaminated soil
ENVO:00002116 · HabitatMech ↗

Four-strain core endophytic synthetic bacterial community (SynCom-NS)—composed of Pseudomonas izuensis SaPI1, Leifsonia shinshuensis SaLS1, Brucella anthropi (Ochrobactrum anthropi) SaOA1, and Novosphingobium lindaniclasticum SaNL1 originally isolated from the hyperaccumulator Sedum alfredii—applied to the high-biomass crop Brassica juncea under cadmium stress. SynCom-NS increases soil Cd bioavailability, upregulates root HMA4 metal transporter expression and glutathione-mediated antioxidant defenses, increases plant fresh weight by 82%, and amplifies shoot Cd accumulation 4.07-fold.

Synergistic Endophytic HMA4 Regulation and Root-to-Shoot Cadmium Translocation
