Wheat Synthetic Consortium C6

Community ID CommunityMech:000077
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment rhizosphere
ENVO:00005801

A four-member mixed-genus wheat-rhizosphere synthetic consortium (SynCom 6/C6) composed of Streptomyces sp. B6, Chryseobacterium sp. B7, Pseudomonas sp. B12, and Sphingomonas sp. B17. Yin et al. reported that C6 inhibited Rhizoctonia solani AG8 radial growth in dual culture and produced auxin in vitro as a whole consortium.
