Magnetite-Mediated Thiobacillus Sulfur-Cycle Phenol and Chromate Bioreactor

Community ID CommunityMech:000493
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment anaerobic bioreactor
ENVO:00002124 · HabitatMech ↗

Magnetite-supplemented anaerobic sulfur-cycle bioreactor community enriched in chemolithotrophic sulfur-oxidizing Thiobacillus species for simultaneous anaerobic phenol degradation and hexavalent chromium (Cr(VI)) reduction via Fe(III)/Fe(II) transformation. Magnetite serves as an Fe(III) source that enriches Thiobacillus species to oxidize elemental sulfur to sulfate, completing the microbial sulfur cycle, increasing aggregate sulfur retention (6.85% vs 4.49% without magnetite), and lowering effluent Cr(VI) (18.0 mg/L vs 187.7 mg/L) and phenol (354.2 mgCOD/L vs 1256.0 mgCOD/L) under low-concentration sulfate conditions (doi:10.1016/j.cej.2020.124801).

Magnetite-Driven Thiobacillus Oxidation of Elemental Sulfur to Sulfate

SYNTROPHY

Magnetite serves as an Fe(III) source that enriches chemolithotrophic Thiobacillus species to oxidize elemental sulfur to sulfate, increasing aggregate sulfur content (6.85% with magnetite vs 4.49% without magnetite) and completing the whole microbial sulfur cycle.

Source Taxon: Thiobacillus

Metabolites: elemental sulfur (CHEBI:33403 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), sulfate (CHEBI:16189 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

Downstream Effects:
→ Sulfur-Cycle-Coupled Anaerobic Phenol Degradation and Cr(VI) Reduction

Completion of the elemental sulfur-to-sulfate cycle by magnetite-enriched Thiobacillus sustains sulfate availability and drives coupled anaerobic phenol degradation (354.2 vs 1256.0 mgCOD/L effluent) and Cr(VI) reduction (18.0 vs 187.7 mg/L effluent).

Sulfur-Cycle-Coupled Anaerobic Phenol Degradation and Cr(VI) Reduction

SYNTROPHY

Whole microbial sulfur cycling in the magnetite-amended anaerobic bioreactor significantly lowers effluent phenol (354.2 mgCOD/L vs 1256.0 mgCOD/L without magnetite) and Cr(VI) (18.0 mg/L vs 187.7 mg/L without magnetite) under low-concentration sulfate conditions while driving dissimilatory Fe(III) reduction of crystalline magnetite.

Source Taxon: Thiobacillus

Metabolites: phenol (CHEBI:15882 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), sulfate (CHEBI:16189 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), chromate(2-) (CHEBI:35404 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), iron(3+) (CHEBI:29034 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)

Biological Processes:

  • phenol-containing compound catabolic process (GO:0019336 · PathwayMech)
  • dissimilatory sulfate reduction (GO:0019420 · PathwayMech)
Downstream Effects:
→ Dissimilatory Fe(II)-Mediated Dissolved Oxygen Scavenging and Cr(III) Stabilization

Dissimilatory Fe(III) reduction of crystalline magnetite under sulfate-reducing conditions generates Fe(II) that reacts with trace dissolved oxygen (DO) to prevent re-oxidation of reduced Cr(III).

Dissimilatory Fe(II)-Mediated Dissolved Oxygen Scavenging and Cr(III) Stabilization

SYNTROPHY

Fe(II) generated via dissimilatory Fe(III) reduction of magnetite reacts with slight dissolved oxygen (DO) in the sulfate-reducing aggregates, preventing re-oxidation of reduced Cr(III) and completing the Fe(III)/Fe(II) redox cycle.

Source Taxon: Thiobacillus

Metabolites: iron(2+) (CHEBI:29033 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), iron(3+) (CHEBI:29034 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech), chromate(2-) (CHEBI:35404 · MediaIngredientMech · CMMMech)
