Growth Media

R2A (complex medium)

Temperature 30 °C
Atmosphere AEROBIC

Preparation notes: R2A selected as the complex medium (a parallel MOPS-glucose minimal medium was also tested); community mixed at approximately equal OD600 and serially passaged every 48 h at a 1:10 dilution for 15 passages. Member strains are obligate aerobes or facultative anaerobes and shaking ensured aeration.
