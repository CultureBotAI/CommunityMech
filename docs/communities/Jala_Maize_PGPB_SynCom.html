SynCom 3 Multi-Trait PGP Activity

The mixed SynCom 3 inoculant produced positive phosphate-solubilization, indole-reactive colorimetric, ACC-growth, metal-chelation and acetylene-reduction assay results. Salkowski chemistry is not specific identification of IAA; growth with ACC as sole nitrogen source is an indirect deaminase screen. Acetylene reduction is a nitrogenase proxy, not measured nitrogen transfer to maize, and the CAS assay does not identify a particular metallophore. These positive in-vitro traits were not selectively perturbed as mediators of plant growth. The four canonical participants are a subset of the reported six-strain SynCom 3, not a complete four-member inoculant; exact membership and the primary Pseudomonas strain inconsistency remain under #1521.

SynCom 3 Antifungal and PAL Induction

SynCom 3 inhibited several tested fungi in vitro, reaching up to 80 percent in the reported comparisons, but Helminthosporium was hardly inhibited by SynCom 3. It also tripled maize-root phenylalanine ammonia-lyase activity. These are positive but distinct assays: the authors did not test induced systemic resistance with a fungal challenge. PAL induction and inhibition of these cultured fungi do not by themselves demonstrate the mechanism of rust reduction in the field. The four canonical participants are a subset of the reported six-strain SynCom 3, not a complete four-member inoculant; exact membership and the primary Pseudomonas strain inconsistency remain under #1521.

Participating Taxa: Pseudomonas protegens, Pantoea ananatis, Klebsiella variicola, Burkholderia sp.

Conejo Maize Seedling Growth Promotion

SynCom 3 inoculation increased Conejo maize growth in gnotobiotic tubes and greenhouse pots. Gnotobiotic root and shoot sizes were 4.2 and 3.0 times the controls. In greenhouse comparisons, root/shoot lengths were 3.3/1.4 times controls and shoot/root dry weights were 2.3/3 times controls. These are positive whole-inoculant treatment effects in the Conejo landrace, not proof that its earlier in-vitro trait panel caused them or that those seedlings produced the separate hybrid field harvest. The four canonical participants are a subset of the reported six-strain SynCom 3, not a complete four-member inoculant; exact membership and the primary Pseudomonas strain inconsistency remain under #1521.

Field Yield Increase and Rust Reduction

In the reported one-site field experiment with CPL9105W hybrid maize, SynCom 3 treatment increased grain yield from 11 to 16 tons/ha relative to control. The abstract reports rust incidence falling from 12.5 to 8 percent, not a 12.5-percentage-point reduction. The treatment involved seed inoculation and subsequent applications; it is distinct from the Conejo seedling experiments. The whole-inoculant effects do not establish which traits or members mediated them, universal efficacy across sites/seasons, or a benefit for every tested SynCom. The four canonical participants are a subset of the reported six-strain SynCom 3, not a complete four-member inoculant; exact membership and the primary Pseudomonas strain inconsistency remain under #1521.
