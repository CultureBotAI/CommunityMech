Caragana-Wheat Drought SynCom15

Modeled Environment Caragana korshinskii root endosphere
ENVO:01001001

A fifteen-member bacterial synthetic community constructed from drought-responsive core microbes of the desert shrub Caragana korshinskii and tested as a cross-host wheat drought-tolerance inoculant. Hao et al. combined four functional strains with eleven core microbial strains, assayed the full SynCom15 in potted plant experiments, and linked SynCom inoculation to wheat growth under drought, Pseudomonas-associated biofilm and quorum-sensing functions, and later streamlining into a biofilm-forming SynCom5.
