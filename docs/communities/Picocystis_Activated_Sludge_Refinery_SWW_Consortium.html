A laboratory algae-bacteria consortium combining Picocystis sp. with an aerobic activated-sludge bacterial consortium to treat petroleum refinery stripped wastewater (SWW). The study compared Picocystis monoculture and Picocystis plus bacteria in 2 L photobioreactors containing 10% or 25% SWW. The Pico-Bac consortium reached 72% COD reduction and 96.4% polyphenol reduction in 10% SWW, while hydrocarbon removal was highest at 80.4% in 25% SWW.
