Model Soil Consortium-2 (MSC-2)

Community ID CommunityMech:000086
Ecological State ENGINEERED
Environment grassland soil
ENVO:00001998

Reduced-complexity model soil consortium assembled from MSC-1 isolates to study community-level chitin decomposition, interspecies interactions, and emergent phenotypes in soil. MSC-2 contains eight bacterial members spanning diverse phyla and was used for isolate genome sequencing, 16S profiling, metatranscriptomics, metabolomics, and KBase-enabled metabolic modeling in the Hofmockel Soil Microbiome SFA context.

Growth Media

M9 minimal medium (18 mM NH4Cl) with colloidal chitin

Temperature 20 °C

Preparation notes: Each strain precultured on R2A agar for 3 days, washed and resuspended in M9 (18 mM NH4Cl); community grown on colloidal shrimp-shell chitin as the sole carbon source (100 ppm for growth screens, 500 ppm for the community multiomics flasks); five biological-replicate flasks (30 mL M9) incubated at 20°C for 5 days.
