Ogataea Str3-Leu2 Auxotrophic Cross-Feeding Coculture

Community ID CommunityMech:000535

ENVO:01001405 · HabitatMech ↗

A defined two-member Ogataea polymorpha synthetic yeast coculture pairing a sulfur assimilation cystathionine beta-lyase auxotroph (str3Δ) with a leucine biosynthesis auxotroph (leu2Δ) constructed in the JQcr03L laboratory background by Fan et al. 2026 (PMID:42318495). Alongside the met10Δ-str3Δ pair (CommunityMech:000450), str3Δ-leu2Δ was identified in systematic pairwise auxotroph screening and shake-flask validation (1:1 strain ratio, initial total OD600 = 0.2, Delft minimal medium containing 20 g/L glucose, 37 degrees C, 220 rpm, n = 4) as one of the two strongly complementary pairs exhibiting the most reproducible and robust growth complementation, reaching maximum OD600 values of 27.3–28.5 (comparable to the prototrophic control strain FY00 at OD600 = 26.8, p > 0.1) after a prolonged lag phase (p < 10^-4) required for cross-pathway metabolic exchange between sulfur assimilation and leucine biosynthesis.

STR3-LEU2 Cross-Pathway Auxotrophic Complementation and Biomass Restoration on Glucose

MUTUALISM

While individual O. polymorpha auxotrophic single mutants failed to grow in unsupplemented Delft minimal glucose medium (OD600 remaining below 1.2), 1:1 co-cultivation of str3Δ and leu2Δ at initial total OD600 = 0.2 in shake flasks (20 g/L glucose, 37 degrees C, 220 rpm, n = 4) resulted in reproducible and robust growth complementation. Along with met10Δ-str3Δ, str3Δ-leu2Δ reached a maximum OD600 of 27.3–28.5 (comparable to the prototrophic control strain FY00 at OD600 = 26.8, p > 0.1) and outperformed moderately complementary pairs such as met10Δ-leu2Δ and ura3Δ-leu2Δ (maximum OD600 = 24–26, lag phases 70–144 h), while exhibiting a significantly longer lag phase than FY00 (p < 10^-4) reflecting the time required to establish reciprocal amino acid/intermediate exchange.

Participating Taxa: Ogataea polymorpha str3Δ, Ogataea polymorpha leu2Δ
