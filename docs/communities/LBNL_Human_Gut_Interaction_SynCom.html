Pairwise Model Prediction Of Community Dynamics

A generalized Lotka-Volterra model trained on monospecies and pairwise time series predicted much of the tested 11- and 12-member community dynamics; additional full-community training improved predictions. PARTIAL - model success supports important pairwise effects in these conditions, not absence of higher-order effects, perfect prediction or universal acetate cross-feeding. Abundance inputs combined 16S proportions with OD600, not direct viable-cell counts.

Inferred Positive-Negative Ecological Network Topology

The fitted 12-species gut network contains frequent negative and positive interaction coefficients, including coupled positive/negative pairwise motifs. These are effective ecological effects inferred from abundance dynamics. Their signs do not uniquely identify nutrient competition, cross-feeding, niche partitioning or molecular mechanisms; conditioned-medium and metabolomics results provide complementary but imperfect support.

Modeled Pairwise Coexistence Feedback

The fitted CH-ER pairwise gLV model contains a positive CH-to-ER coefficient and a negative ER-to-CH coefficient, forming the source's model-bounded negative-feedback coexistence motif (Figure4B/C). Source/target name the positive limb; the reverse inhibitory limb is part of the coupled motif. CH is Peptacetobacter hiranonis DSM13275 (paper Clostridium hiranonis); ER is Agathobacter rectalis ATCC33656 (paper Eubacterium rectale). The model comparison supports coexistence across broader tested growth-rate regimes than mutual inhibition, not a demonstrated molecular mediator or a feedback involving all twelve members.

Knowledge gaps & discussions (2)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which molecular mechanisms realize the inferred signed interactions and feedback?

Remove the analytical inference-to-topology arrow, but retain topology-to-coexistence as a model-bounded causal mechanism. Acetate in the cited passage is a prior-literature example; separate metabolomics cultures supplemented ER and FP with acetate, which does not prove cross-feeding throughout this graph. Co-consumption/secretion only yields an ecological effect when relevant substrates limit growth, and conditioned-medium signs do not always match inferred coefficients. Higher-order effects, parameter uncertainty and OD-scaled abundance remain limitations. At the October 6, 2026 review checkpoint, the record still used one aggregate bacterial entry instead of the experimental twelve-stock roster; #1548 tracked that historical grounding and non-graph metadata gap. That review had not inspected original figure pixels or separate supplements, and had not reanalysed raw data or reproduced the executable model. The November 15 reassessment subsequently grounded the twelve Methods stocks and inspected the complete article text/captions, Figure 4B/C pixels, Appendix and EV1-EV4. Raw-data reanalysis, all supplemental figure pixels and executable model reproduction remain outside the reassessment's scope.

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which stock-resolved molecular transfers explain the model-inferred signed interactions?

The November2026 reassessment replaces the old aggregate roster and unsupported acetate-mediator assertion. Main Methods, Appendix, EV1-EV4 and stock grounding were inspected. Monospecies metabolite depletion/secretion and conditioned-medium responses nominate mechanisms but do not demonstrate direct transfer between named stocks. Acetate addition is confined to the ER/FP metabolomics protocol; it does not establish a community-wide mediator. CA GTDB is unresolved and PC has two crosswalk candidates; no individual stock genome is assigned. The October discussion is now explicitly scoped to its historical checkpoint; its original wording is preserved in the prior Git and review artifacts.
