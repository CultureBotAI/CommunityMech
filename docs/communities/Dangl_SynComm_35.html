A 35-member synthetic community (SynComm 35) composed of genome-sequenced bacterial strains representing typical taxonomic diversity of Arabidopsis root commensals. This community was designed to interrogate plant immune responses, particularly pattern-triggered immunity (PTI/MTI). Of the 35 members, 10 are robust suppressors of MAMP-triggered immunity (flg22 response), 13 are partial suppressors, and 11 are non-suppressors. The community includes 32 strains isolated from Brassicaceae roots (mainly Arabidopsis), 2 strains from unplanted soil, and E. coli DH5α as a control. SynComm 35 collectively suppresses specific transcriptional sectors of plant immunity, particularly WRKY transcription factors, receptor-like kinases, and secondary metabolite biosynthesis genes, while suppressors achieve enhanced colonization capacity.

Root flg22 Immune Suppression

Live SynCom35 suppresses a sector of the Arabidopsis root flg22 response after establishment. Dyella MF79 is one tested suppressor, not the sole cause of whole-community transcriptional changes. Participants name the host and this canonical member, not the complete 35-strain roster.

Participating Taxa: Arabidopsis thaliana, Dyella japonica MF79

Biological Processes:

  • pattern recognition receptor signaling pathway (GO:0002221)
  • defense response to bacterium (GO:0042742)
  • regulation of innate immune response (GO:0045088)
Downstream Effects:
→ Suppression-Associated Root Colonization Facilitation

Suppression can facilitate nonsuppressor root colonization under the tested conditions. MF79 wild-type retentate, unlike T2SS-mutant material, countered the flg22-associated reduction in Ochrobactrum MF370 colonization. This does not establish universal facilitation by every suppressor or reciprocal benefit.

Community-Associated WRKY Transcript Suppression

SynCom35 suppressed flg22-responsive WRKY transcripts. The host and MF79 participants represent a known subset of the inoculated system, not a demonstrated MF79-specific WRKY mechanism. A regulatory role in colonization homeostasis remains proposed.

MF79 Type II Secretion-Dependent Immune Modulation

MF79 secretion-system mutants and cell-free supernatants support T2SS-dependent suppression of the root flg22 response; T3SS was dispensable. The active secreted molecule remains unresolved. This strain-specific requirement is not assigned to every SynCom35 member.

Source Taxon: Dyella japonica MF79

Target Taxon: Arabidopsis thaliana

Biological Processes:

  • protein secretion (GO:0009306)
  • modulation by symbiont of host defense response (GO:0052031)
Downstream Effects:
→ Root flg22 Immune Suppression

MF79 T2SS function enables its extracellular suppressive activity in the root reporter assay. This arrow applies to the MF79 component of the suppression node, not necessity of T2SS for the entire SynCom35 phenotype.

Suppression-Associated Root Colonization Facilitation

COLONIZATION_FACILITATION
