Maize SC2 Root-Rot Biocontrol SynCom

A three-member maize-root synthetic microbial community refined from the C7 corn root consortium to improve Pythium torulosum biocontrol while preserving plant growth promotion. Kaki et al. selected SC2 as S4, S1, and S5: Enterobacter cloacae S4, Stenotrophomonas maltophilia S1, and Chryseobacterium indologenes S5. Plate assays showed P. torulosum inhibition comparable to C7, growth-chamber pot assays showed stronger maize protection and growth than C7 under P. torulosum challenge, and metabolite assays showed that SC2 had the highest measured indole-3-acetic acid production among C7, SC1, SC2, and SC3 together with significant siderophore activity on CAS agar.

Knowledge gaps & discussions (1)

KNOWLEDGE_GAP OPEN Which SC2 member metabolites or community-level interactions directly suppress Pythium torulosum and connect the strong IAA and siderophore phenotypes to maize protection?

Kaki et al. 2026 showed that the S4/S1/S5 subcommunity inhibited Pythium in plate assays, protected maize in growth-chamber assays, and produced high IAA plus significant siderophore activity, but the specific antimicrobials, responsible members, and causal links among siderophores, auxin, root growth, and oomycete suppression remain unresolved.
