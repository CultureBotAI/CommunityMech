A synthetic model lignocellulose-degrading community developed to study plant-biomass transformation and mitigation of toxic formaldehyde produced during breakdown of methoxylated aromatic compounds. The system includes Pseudomonas putida as a lignin degrader, Cellulomonas fimi as a cellulose degrader, Methylorubrum extorquens as a methylotrophic formaldehyde consumer, and Yarrowia lipolytica as an oleaginous yeast included in some consortium variants.
