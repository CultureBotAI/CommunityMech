Cached source notes:
- The article profiles KMC-IMBG1, a Ukrainian kombucha microbial community, by culture-dependent
  isolation and 16S/ITS metabarcoding.
- It compares sterile sweetened black tea, nonsterile sweetened black tea, honey-supplemented black
  tea, and a cabbage-brine hybrid microenvironment.
- It reports a core acetobacterial and yeast community plus accessory members that vary with growth
  conditions.
