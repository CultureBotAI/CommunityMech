Cached source notes:
- The article reports a four-species mammalian-gut consortium engineered for amino acid auxotrophy and
  overproduction.
- The engineered consortium was tested in anaerobic modified M9 batch culture and in gnotobiotic mice
  fed standard or low-protein chow.
- Strain abundances were tracked with strain-specific qPCR and used to quantify population evenness.
