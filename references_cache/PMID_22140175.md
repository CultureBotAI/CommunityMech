# Rhodanobacter denitrificans sp. nov., isolated from nitrate-rich zones of a contaminated aquifer

## Cached Evidence Snippets

Bacterial strains 2APBS1(T) and 116-2 were isolated from the subsurface of a nuclear legacy waste site where the sediments are co-contaminated with large amounts of acids, nitrate, metal radionuclides and other heavy metals.

these strains represent the first member of the genus Rhodanobacter shown to be capable of complete denitrification.

the name Rhodanobacter denitrificans sp. nov. is proposed. The type strain is 2APBS1(T) ( = DSM 23569(T) = JCM 17641(T)).
