# PMID:29087377

Title: Degradation of hydrogen peroxide at the ocean's surface: the influence of the microbial community on the realized thermal niche of Prochlorococcus

Source: ISME Journal, 2018.

Key evidence used:

- Prochlorococcus is sensitive to hydrogen peroxide.
- Alteromonas and other heterotrophs facilitate Prochlorococcus growth by scavenging HOOH.
- Heterotrophic bacteria expanded the permissive temperature range for Prochlorococcus growth.
