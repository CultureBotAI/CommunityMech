# Influence of tryptophan and indole-3-acetic acid on starch accumulation in the synthetic mutualistic Chlorella sorokiniana–Azospirillum brasilense system under heterotrophic conditions
**Authors:** Oskar A. Palacios, Francisco J. Choix, Yoav Bashan, Luz E. de-Bashan
**Journal:** Research in Microbiology (2016)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.resmic.2016.02.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resmic.2016.02.005)

## Content

This study measured the relations between tryptophan production, the phytohormone indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) and the metabolism and accumulation of starch during synthetic mutualism between the microalgae Chlorella sorokiniana and the microalgae growth-promoting bacteria Azospirillum brasilense, created by co-immobilization in alginate beads. Experiments used two wild-type A. brasilense strains (Cd and Sp6) and an IAA-attenuated mutant (SpM7918) grown under nitrogen-replete and nitrogen-starved conditions tested under dark, heterotrophic and aerobic growth conditions.
