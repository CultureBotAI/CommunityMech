1. Water Environ Res. 2026 Jul;98(7):e70489. doi: 10.1002/wer.70489.

Activated Microalgae-Activated MLSS Bacteria Consortium Synergistically Improves
Phycoremediation Kinetics: A Parallel Bio-Activation Approach for Effective
Wastewater Treatment and Biomass Production.

Kumar GU(1), Antony LM(1), Jacob J(1), Jayakumar AV(1), Das D(1), Kuncheria
L(1), Rao PH(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology, Madras Christian College (Affiliated to the
University of Madras), Chennai, India.

Rapid and efficient wastewater treatment processes with valuable resource
recovery are the need of the hour for developing robust, sustainable, and
integrated biological systems. This study introduces a novel parallel
bioactivation approach combining Chlorella vulgaris and mixed liquor suspended
solids (MLSSs) bacterial consortium for wastewater treatment in an outdoor
high-rate algal pond (HRAP). C. vulgaris was activated separately in suboptimal
nutrient concentrations (0.1×) with respect to the standard commercial medium
for 2 days before treatment. Separately activated bacterial MLSS was
incorporated into activated C. vulgaris, making an AM-AB consortium. The AM-AB
consortium demonstrated enhanced nutrient remediation with nitrogen removal
efficiency of 99.9% after 6-h treatment and complete removal of phosphate
(< 0.01 mg L-1) in 2 h of treatment. The maximum specific removal rate for total
nitrogen reached 49.81 mg g-1 h-1, whereas ammonia showed high substrate
affinity (Ks = 0.53 mg L-1). The consortium achieved 81.73% BOD reduction,
indicating the synergistic interaction between microalgae and bacteria,
supporting the potential of the AM-AB consortium for biological wastewater
treatment.
