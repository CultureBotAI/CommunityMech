# doi:10.1038/ismej.2017.189

Title: The impact of elevated CO2 on Prochlorococcus and microbial interactions with 'helper' bacterium Alteromonas

Source: The ISME Journal / Nature, 2018.

Key evidence used:

- Prochlorococcus is described as a marine cyanobacterium that lacks catalase and relies on helper bacteria.
- The study cocultured Prochlorococcus strains VOL4 and MIT9312 with Alteromonas strain EZ55 under 400 ppm and 800 ppm CO2.
- Alteromonas catalase expression and hydrogen peroxide removal decreased under elevated CO2.
