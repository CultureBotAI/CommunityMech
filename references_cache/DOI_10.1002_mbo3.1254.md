- The article describes construction and perturbation testing of a four-species model biofilm derived
  from an industrial milk pasteurization-line biofilm.
- The model contains Rhodocyclus sp., Pseudomonas fluorescens, Kocuria varians, and Bacillus cereus.
- The paper reports dynamic positive and negative interactions and identifies Bacillus cereus thiocillin I
  as a molecular determinant of one negative interaction.
