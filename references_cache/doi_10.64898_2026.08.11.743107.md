## Content

An engineered Clostridial mini consortium modulates intestinal inflammation

Combining R. gnavus (WT or KO) with Peptacetobacter hiranonis created a two-member consortium

a consortium of P. hiranonis and R. gnavus WT (isoDCA+ consortium) can produce isoDCA

R. gnavus (VPI C7-9, originally acquired from ATCC (29149)

10 mL of P. hiranonis culture (OD600 = 0.800) was mixed with 10 mL of R. gnavus WT or KO culture

Resulting pellet was aspirated and resuspended in 1mL sterile BHI-S medium supplemented with 15% (v/v) sterile glycerol

Fecal isoDCA concentration

Abundance of both P. hiranonis and R. gnavus were checked before, during, and after the colonization period by qPCR with species-specific primers

isoDCA can induce both ileal and colonic lamina propria (LP) RORγt + Foxp3 + pTregs

isoDCA-producing (isoDCA+) consortium protects from colitis in the CD45RB hi adoptive T cell transfer model

Schematic of the conversion of TCA into CA and DCA by P. hiranonis and into isoDCA by R. gnavus

P. hiranonis was cultured in vitro with TCA and then with either R. gnavus WT or R. gnavus KO

were cultured at 37 °C in an incubator inside a flexible vinyl anaerobic chamber

flexible vinyl anaerobic chamber with concentrations of H2 = 2.5-3.0% and O2 < 50 ppm

supplemented brain heart infusion (BHI-S) medium consisting of 37 g/L of brain heart infusion powder (BD Difco), 5 g/L yeast extract (BD Bacto), and 1% (v/v) of 10% (w/v) L-cysteine (Sigma)

secondary cultures in BHI-S medium that were grown at 37 °C for 12-16 hours to OD 600 ≈ 0.800

yeast casitone fatty acids broth with carbohydrates (YCFAC) medium

secondary YCFAC cultures were spiked with 8 μL of 100 mM bile acids prior to reduction in the anaerobic chamber

Secondary cultures spiked with bile acids were inoculated with 50 μL of P. hiranonis, R. gnavus WT, or R. gnavus KO turbid primary cultures and grown at 37 °C overnight

avoid sterol compounds found in BHI-S medium
