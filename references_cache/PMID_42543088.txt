Antimicrobial aromatic Butenolides and anthraquinone derivative produced in
cocultures of Cordyceps militaris ZYb8 and Malbranchea ostraviensis SS1.

Yang H(1), Luo Q(1), Yang J(1), Zhang Y(1), Zhang X(2).

Author information:
(1)College of Chinese Pharmacy, Yunnan University of Chinese Medicine, Kunming
650500, China.
(2)College of Chinese Pharmacy, Yunnan University of Chinese Medicine, Kunming
650500, China; College of Basic Medicine, Yunnan University of Chinese Medicine,

Integrated with LC-MS/MS-based molecular networking analysis, eight previously
undescribed compounds-eutypoid G-K (1-5), gotjawalides A-B (6-7), and
ocauxarthrol B (8)-along with 11 known natural products (9-19) were isolated
from the coculture of Cordyceps militaris ZYb8 and Malbranchea ostraviensis SS1.
The structures of those previously undescribed compounds (1-8) were elucidated
through comprehensive spectroscopic data analyses, including NMR, HRESIMS, UV,
and IR. The absolute configurations of compounds 6-8 were assigned by comparing
experimental electronic circular dichroism (ECD) spectra with theoretically
calculated spectra. Antimicrobial assays revealed that compounds 1-13, 15-16,
and 19 exhibited weak to moderate selective antibacterial activities against S.
aureus, B. subtilis, S. pneumoniae, and K. pneumoniae with MIC values ranging
from 4 to 128 μg/mL.
