# PMID:12523427

Title: Field demonstration of successful bioaugmentation to achieve dechlorination of tetrachloroethene to ethene

Source: Environmental Science & Technology, 2002.

Key evidence used:

- KB-1 is a natural dechlorinating microbial consortium containing Dehalococcoides-related organisms.
- Field bioaugmentation converted chlorinated ethenes to ethene after electron donor amendment.
- Dehalococcoides sequences from KB-1 increased and spread after bioaugmentation.
