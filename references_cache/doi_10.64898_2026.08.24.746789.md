## Content

Ecologically inspired assembly of a Bacillales synthetic community for lignin degradation

121 strains used in this work are from the order Bacillales

M9 minimal medium was used to provide a nutrient-limited environment

promote the utilization of lignin as the main carbon source

the final lignin concentration of 1 g/L

0.2% of glucose was included to support initial bacterial growth

All cultures were incubated in flasks at 30 °C using 200 rpm shaking

100 μL of each culture was inoculated into M9-lignin medium shake flasks

LDSynCom was cultivated in M9 minimal medium containing 1 g/L lignin

The LDSyncom was inoculated into M9 medium and M9 supplemented with lignin, with four biological replicates per condition

deposited in NCBI Bioproject under PRJNA1505615

assembled complete genomes under NCBI Bioproject PRJNA960711

ACCC04323, LY18, YX13 and DX1.1 showed poor biofilm formation and/or low dye decolorization

a total 11 strains were shown to produce biofilm or decolorize lignin-like dyes

well-developed biofilm structures could be observed attached to the surface of lignin particles

Removing strain 11B from the LDSynCom reduced the overall growth

The LDSynCom samples produced significantly higher phenolic signals than the uninoculated control

functional roles within the consortium are not strictly partitioned

All analysis code is available at: (https://github.com/Xinming9606/lignin_SynCom_proteomics)
