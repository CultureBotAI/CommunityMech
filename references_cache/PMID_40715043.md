The Spruce and Peatland Responses Under Changing Environments (SPRUCE) experiment, combines air and peat warming in a whole-ecosystem manipulation experiment which aims to understand the impact of climate change drivers (warming and elevated atmospheric CO_{2}) on ecosystem functioning in a forested peat bog in northern Minnesota.
