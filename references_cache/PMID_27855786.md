Cached source notes:
- The article establishes a two-member co-culture between Emiliania huxleyi CCMP3266 and Phaeobacter
  inhibens DSM 17395.
- It reports physical attachment, algal nutrient support of bacterial growth, and indole-3-acetic
  acid-mediated growth promotion and algal death.
- Tryptophan exuded by the algal cell increases bacterial indole-3-acetic acid production and attachment.
