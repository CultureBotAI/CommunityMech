Title: Mimicking lichens: incorporation of yeast strains together with sucrose-secreting cyanobacteria improves survival, growth, ROS removal, and lipid production in a stable mutualistic co-culture production platform.

Cached source notes:
- The article tests artificial lichen-like cocultures pairing sucrose-secreting Synechococcus elongatus with
  oleaginous yeasts including Rhodotorula glutinis.
- It reports sucrose utilization by yeasts, sustained S. elongatus-R. glutinis coculture, improved biomass
  and lipid yield, and relief of cyanobacterial reactive oxygen stress in coculture.
