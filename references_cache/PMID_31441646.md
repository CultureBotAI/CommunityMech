# PMID:31441646

Title: Sustained Dechlorination of Vinyl Chloride to Ethene in Dehalococcoides-Enriched Cultures Grown without Addition of Exogenous Vitamins and at Low pH

Source: Environmental Science & Technology, 2019.

Key evidence used:

- KB-1 grew in TCE-amended vitamin-free mineral medium and VC-amended low-pH medium.
- Acetobacterium and Sporomusa are dominant acetogens in the cultures.
- Acetobacterium supported complete TCE dechlorination by Dehalococcoides with increased cobalamin.
