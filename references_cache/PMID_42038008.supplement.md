Section S1：
Plasmid construction
Cas9 protein can recognize and cleave the target DNA through the 20 bp complementary region of sgRNA, and all sgRNAs used in this study were designed via ChopChop (https://chopchop.cbu.uib.no). The plasmid pTarget-pMB1 served as the template, and the linearized DNA fragment pTarget-glk was obtained by PCR amplification using primer pairs glk-F1/glk-R1. The sequence of primers is listed in Table S1. To prevent the formation of false-positive transformants after transformation, the DNA template was digested with Dpn I before recombinant cyclization. The cyclization was conducted using ClonExpress Ultra One Step Cloning Kit (Vazyme, China). The resulting cyclized plasmid was transformed into E. coli DH5α and then achieved plasmid preparation. Plasmid that contained another target genes (ptsI, pps, poxB, pflB, pta, frdA, frdB, frdC, adhE, dld, and mgsA) was constructed following the methodology described above.
Acquisition of donor DNA
Bacterial genomes were obtained using the Bacterial Genome Extraction Kit (Solarbio, China). To prepare the donor DNA for knockout of the glk gene, using the genomic DNA of E coli BL21 (DE3) as the template. The primer pairs glk-F2/glk-R2 and glk-F3/glk-R3 (Table S1) were used to amplify the upstream and downstream homology arms of the glk gene by PCR. The donor DNA was then obtained by Overlap Extension PCR to connect the upstream and downstream homologous arms.
Preparation of E. coli competent cells and transformation by electroporation
The plasmid pCas was transformed into E coli BL21 (DE3), and a transformed colony was cultured in LB medium containing 10 g/L tryptone, 5 g/L yeast extract, and 10 g/L NaCl. When the OD600 reached 0.2, L-arabinose was added to the culture at a final concentration of 10 mM. When the OD600 reached 0.6, the bacterial pellets were obtained by high-speed cryo-centrifugation and washed once with pre-cooled sterile water and twice with 10% (v/v) pre-cooled glycerol, then aliquoted and stored at -80°C. The pTarget-glk and the corresponding donor DNA were co-transformed into electrocompetent cells using a 1 mm cuvette at 2.5 kV. After electroporation, 1 mL of pre-cooled LB medium broth was added to the cuvette, and the bacterial cells were recovered at 30°C for 1 h with gentle shaking. Thereafter, 100 µL of bacterial culture was plated on LB agar plates containing 50 mg/L kanamycin and 100 mg/L spectinomycin and incubated at 30°C overnight.
Identification of positive clones and plasmid curing
The positive clones grown on selective plates (containing 50 mg/L kanamycin and 100 mg/L spectinomycin) were verified by colony PCR and Sanger sequencing using primers glk-yz/glk-R3 (Table S1). The positive clones were cultured in LB medium containing a final concentration of 50 mg/L kanamycin and 10 mM IPTG. The cultures were incubated at 30°C with shaking at 200 rpm for 12 h. The single colonies were obtained on kanamycin plate by streak plate method. The colonies were simultaneously transfer onto plate containing either 50 mg/L kanamycin or 100 mg/L spectinomycin. Colonies that could grow only on kanamycin plates indicated successful elimination of pTarget. To eliminate plasmid pCas, the strains that successfully eliminated pTarget were incubated at 37℃ with shaking at 200 rpm. The single colonies were also obtained on LB plate by streak plate method. The colonies were simultaneously transfer onto plate with and without the additional of 50 mg/L kanamycin, respectively. The plates were cultured at 37℃ overnight. The colonies could grow only on LB plate were considered as the strains that successfully eliminated pCas.

Section S2:
qRT-PCR analysis

Primers are listed in Table S2. 16S rRNA gene was used as an internal control for normalization.
