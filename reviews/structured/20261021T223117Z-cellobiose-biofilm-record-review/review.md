## Findings

### align-citrate-identity: Reconcile citrate chemical identity

major / open / confirmed; issue key: cm-000196-align-citrate-identity.

The medium sodium-citrate ingredient and the citrate metabolite are grounded to neutral citric acid, CHEBI:30769. Exact label matching does not make these chemical forms equivalent. Reconcile the formulation and metabolic pool separately. Methods reports 3.0 g/L Na-Citrate but no hydration or precise sodium stoichiometry. CHEBI:53258 is the verified trisodium salt, not an automatically justified generic salt; CHEBI:16947 is citrate(3−), not proof of exclusive solution protonation. Use a supported form or explicitly retain unresolved salt precision rather than silently substitute citric acid or invent a hydrate.

The shared growth block combines anaerobic, 150 rpm, 72-hour liquid settings with distinct biofilm regimes. Preserve 100 µL biofilm inoculation and starting OD600 0.01/0.001 as different quantities, not a viable-cell ratio. Keep CSP, M9 necromass assays and analytical dispersal/lysis conditions separate. Preserve source-reported unusual resazurin concentration without inventing a corrected recipe.
