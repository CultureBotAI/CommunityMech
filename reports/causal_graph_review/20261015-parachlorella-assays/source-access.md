## Assay interpretation

Methods 2.1.2 and Figure 3 identify species-resolved P. kessleri cell counts.
At day 12, the reported coculture and enriched monoculture counts are 4.8 and
2.8 times the unsupplemented BBM + NO3 control. The medium-matched comparison
supports additional yeast-associated growth; 4.8-fold is not the isolated yeast
effect. The source strains are UTEX 2229 and BY4741 (Methods 2.1).

Methods 2.3 instead uses a day-14 pellet, resuspended at a fixed cell density,
with bulk Nile Red plate fluorescence. It does not describe species separation
for this endpoint. Results 3.2 reports 3437 +/- 17 and 14195 +/- 216 arbitrary
units for control and coculture, and separately calls the latter 4.3-fold.
Those means divide to approximately 4.13, not 4.3. Methods specifies 2 micrograms/mL
dye whereas Results specifies 2.5. Preserve these source discrepancies;
do not turn relative fluorescence into lipid mass, volumetric productivity,
or an exclusively algal lipid measurement. Supplementary Figure S2 remains
uninspected. No measurement is rejected solely because the reporting conflicts.

Growth maintenance after day 6 is observed; gas exchange and nutrient competition
are mechanistic interpretations, not isolated mediation estimates. AFM/FluidFM
support physical observations, not demonstrated EPS-carbon uptake. Main-text
Methods do not describe direct gas-flux or EPS-uptake tracing; this is a bounded
main-text assessment, not a claim about unavailable supplements. No new node or
arrow is added. Issue #1681 remains open for the remaining source/provider work.
