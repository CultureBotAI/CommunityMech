## 7. Representation, Writer, and Offline-Proteome Boundaries

1. **Canonical Location of `UniProtKB:` Protein Grounding (`CommonTaxon.genes`)**:
   In `src/communitymech/schema/communitymech.yaml`, `GeneAnnotation` (`gene_id`, `gene_symbol`, `locus_tag`, `product`, `genome`, `kegg_ortholog`, `go_terms`, `supports_roles`, `supports_interaction`, `evidence`) is defined exclusively on `CommonTaxon` (`kb/taxa/*.yaml`). Community and isolate records (`MicrobialCommunity` in `kb/communities/*.yaml` and `data/isolates/*.yaml`) link to reusable taxon records via `TaxonomicComposition.common_taxon` and represent mechanism graphs via `EcologicalInteraction` (`biological_processes`, `metabolites`, `downstream`, `evidence`).
2. **Guarded Writer Contract (`write_validated_community`)**:
   `write_validated_community(doc, path, target_class="CommonTaxon")` in `src/communitymech/validation/write_validated.py` enforces closed-schema LinkML validation (`JsonschemaValidationPlugin(closed=True)`) on `CommonTaxon` records before writing.
3. **Offline-Proteome Boundary**:
   Network calls to external UniProt REST endpoints are prohibited under repository rules when curating in the sandboxed environment, and `data/raw/` currently stores GTDB (`NCBI2GTDB.tsv.gz`), MediaDive, and NCBI taxonomy dumps without UniProt reference proteome TSV tables for Cohort 4 organisms. Consequently, Cohort 4 gene symbols and locus tags are preserved in mechanism-graph prose/notes and documented above rather than assigned unverified `UniProtKB:` accessions.
